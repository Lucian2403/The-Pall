## Approved game description — owner-edited

The Pall is a free-to-play, persistent browser, text-based MMORPG focused on character progression, exploration, crafting, trading and a player-driven economy.

Develop your skills without fixed classes, pursue different professions, undertake interactive jobs and prepare for expeditions into dangerous territories. Gameplay combines illustrated, text-driven interactions with tactical turn-based combat, where preparation, equipment and decisions matter.

The world continues to evolve, and your choices determine how you make your living, what you specialise in and how far you're willing to venture.

**No pay-to-win mechanics. No mandatory PvP. No fixed classes.**

*The Pall is currently in active development, with new gameplay systems and content being introduced progressively.*
