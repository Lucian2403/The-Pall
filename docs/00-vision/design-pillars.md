# The Pall — Core Design Pillars

## Core fantasy

Civilization survives inside protected industrial cities while the hazardous world beyond remains partially unknown.

> There will always be somewhere beyond the map.

## Core loop

City → choose objective → work/train/trade → choose outside objective → prepare → Grey Marches / Ashlands → encounter, discovery, puzzle or ambush → return or continue deeper → heal, sell, craft, rest, research or trade → repeat.

## Locked principles

- Attributes create potential; skills create competence.
- Equipment is the primary survival layer.
- Pall/Ash resistance comes from equipment, consumables and rare foods, not raw attributes.
- No class walls. Practical specialization comes from time, skills, facilities, equipment, reputation and infrastructure.
- PvP is voluntary by default.
- The economy needs permanent sinks.
- Reputation gates access and may also be spendable.
- The world must remain partially unknown.
- Systems expose generic inputs and outputs instead of hard-coded one-off dependencies.
- The game should remain extensible: new content should primarily be data, not new special-case code.
