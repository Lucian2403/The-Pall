# The Plume — Historical Catastrophe and Regions

**Status: OWNER-APPROVED LORE FOUNDATION (2026-10-26).** The ultimate cause, scientific mechanism and exact biological effects are **not established**. This file documents agreed setting facts, not a quest revelation or a new exposure formula.

## The Plume

Approximately **80–100 years** before the game's present, a massive worldwide geological event caused enormous ruptures that released toxic fumes, dust and unfamiliar substances. Older people still call the event **The Plume**.

The resulting contamination altered the land, soil and living organisms, affecting humans, flora and fauna. **How** individual organisms changed, and the precise medical and ecological consequences, remain open questions; do not invent universal visible mutations or fixed effects on character statistics.

The catastrophe and its aftermath involved hunger, loss of shelter, displaced populations and fear of dangerous, uncharted territory. While very few living people could recall its earliest days, older people can remember hardships that continued afterward.

People have since developed competing explanations—some geological, some concerned with human activity, and others religious or speculative. **No explanation is confirmed.** The true cause is deliberately unresolved.

## Regions and playable progression

1. **Grand Cylinder City and Protected Outskirts:** population, work, industry, food production and accessible protected land
2. **Grey Marches:** first hazardous frontier and exploration region
3. **The Pall:** more dangerous, poorly understood territory requiring specialised preparation and equipment

**First playable vertical slice:** city → protected outskirts → Grey Marches → return. **Broader Phase One:** later access to the Pall. This follows the [agreed first-playable scope](../00-vision/first-playable-scope.md) and [Phase One resource audit](../03-resources/catalogue/phase-one-resource-audit.md). The Pall is not available in the first vertical slice.

The term **Ashlands** appears in some older documentation as a progression shorthand. It is **not a separately required second danger zone** in this agreed hierarchy. Do not add it as a mandatory tier or silently delete earlier historical references outside the agreed progression text.

## Separate concepts

- **The Plume:** the historical global event
- **The Pall:** hazardous territory and environmental conditions remaining in the world
- **Grand Cylinder:** the major industrial installation supplying the city with heat and energy, maintained by extensive human labour

The [approved player-facing opening](../05-gameplay/onboarding/world-introduction.md) mentions the Plume without resolving its cause.
