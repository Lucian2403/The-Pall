# Squatter — Protected Outskirts Opening

**Status (2026-10-13):** The starting in the **Protected Outskirts at a rough gathering shelter**, with a **recurring paid foraging contractor**, is agreed in direction. The precise **shelter name, NPC title, first job, dark twist, choice branches and antagonist** below are **PROPOSED** until user approval. The universal [tutorial structure](shared-structure.md), **Fieldcraft +3**, and standard starter kit are already locked.

## Character starting state (LOCKED)

- **Origin:** Squatter — raised among families in the Protected Outskirts; familiar with local survival routines.
- **Background Major Skill:** **Fieldcraft +3** at character creation. Fieldcraft includes Foraging, Scavenging, Navigation, Tracking; the initial job uses **Foraging**.
- **Attributes:** STR 5 / DEX 5 / INT 5 / RES 5; base max Health 50.
- **Starter gear for all origins:** **Basic Clothes, one Crude Small Bag, 20 coins.** No background-special starter knives, medicines or weapons. Exact item records/stats remain TBD.
- **Tutorial:** first Fieldcraft job → unique choice → paid second job in a **different Major Skill** → two interactions → short **turn-based PvE fight** → treatment at ordinary city care service → home with little money → rent notice, E2E tutorial ends. The overarching main storyline does not start here.

## The gathering shelter — proposal for concrete hub identity

**Working name: Hedge Shelter.** A shabby timber-and-canvas lean-to at the **protected outskirts near the Grand Cylinder City gate**. It has marked harvesting routes, hanging herb bundles, a communal scale and a contract board, plus sheltered baskets. Nearby common fields are a low-risk, established Fieldcraft work area. Exact neighbourhood, map tile and asset layout TBD.

**Recurring job NPC: Gathering Factor** (job title, individual name TBD): a practical broker who weighs and pays for ordinary collected plants and assigns nearby Foraging/gathering jobs. This is the permanent **Fieldcraft / Foraging paid-job hub**, not a tutorial-only place or a general city Smithing station. The Factor is morally ambiguous as an employer but not declared a villain.

**First paid job (proposed): Yarrow Cut**. Gather a modest assigned quantity of common **Yarrow** from a reliable known public patch near the shelter. Yarrow's normal safe field/outskirts sourcing and bleeding-control medicinal role are already documented in [Herbs and Harvesting](../../03-resources/raw/herbs-and-harvesting.md).

Normal, reusable Foraging interactions:
1. Speak to the Factor: see the quantity, payment, estimated time and a simple gathering destination.
2. Identify **Yarrow** and confirm the patch, paying attention to safe plant identification.
3. Choose a harvest method / how much to take without ruining regrowth; normal yield, patch health, equipment/time implications apply.
4. Sort dirty/damaged plant matter from usable cuttings and return/deliver through a generic quantity/condition check. This is ordinary Fieldcraft/Foraging XP and job payment, not a one-off minigame.

**Source-of-truth economy guardrail:** Herb material has **no Crude–Masterwork tier**; patch population and harvest pressure are shared-world rules. Introductory required common Yarrow must remain obtainable even when the world patch is depleted or other players have harvested it.

## Twisted tutorial event — proposal: The Warm Patch

**Only after the normal Foraging interactions** are finished, the Squatter discovers that this Yarrow bed is thriving over a half-collapsed outfall beside the old poorhouse disposal ground. Wet ribbons from medical dressings are tangled around the roots; a human finger bone has surfaced after a rain. A faint warmth comes through the drainage bricks. The Factor expected ordinary plants and gives no explanation for the newly exposed remains.

This is a **local scandal concerning neglected remains and the supply of medical herbs**, not a Pall expedition or the opening of the main plot. The buried dead are implied; avoid gratuitous gore or requiring full simulated burial systems. Exact identity/cause and why the drain is warm remain **TBD**, not a new canon Pall phenomenon.

## Two follow-up interactions after second job — proposals

**Interaction 1, "The Rustling Bank":** Returning toward the city gate along the edge of the disturbed drainage ground, the player sees rooted soil shifting and hears scraping among the reeds. Choose whether to inspect tracks, take the raised path or investigate a fresh tear in the ground. The choices determine information/position, not whether the mandatory combat exists. A Fieldcraft-aware player may identify animal signs sooner.

**Interaction 2, "Nowhere to Step":** A **young, injured wild boar**, disturbed by the exposed ditch and human activity, bursts from the reeds into the narrow passage. The player decides whether to hold ground, retreat behind a broken fence section or move toward the stone embankment; then regular turn-based PvE begins. **A young/wounded boar is a PROPOSED opponent, not agreed yet.** Boar already exists in the Phase One animal roster and can injure people even outside Pall zones; beginner combat difficulty must be carefully tuned to the starting 50 Health and lack of a weapon. Do not imply a normal wild boar is an easy bare-handed kill.

### Short fight, care and home — requirements and proposed story

- **Pitched opponent:** the desperate, injured boar, rather than a third disguised human security guard. A small environmental obstacle/defensive option lets an unarmed novice survive; player might drive it off rather than kill it, and receives ordinary combat XP where appropriate. Mechanics, damage and animal AI are to be designed later. If this cannot be balanced fairly without an invented tutorial-specific weapon, **choose a different opponent** at review.
- **Injury and care:** the player suffers a plausible minor wound (e.g., torn forearm from a glancing tusk/abrasion; wounds/bleeding rules TBD), seeks **paid city medical treatment**, learns inspection and patching, then returns home. Do not lock disease or permanent injury mechanics based on this pitch.
- **Ending:** a humble rented room or shelter connected to their outskirts household is reached after treatment; after two small jobs and a clinic bill, only a little money remains. **Rent is due**, and the tutorial ends. Whether a protected-outskirts dwelling can be subject to rent and who collects it need specific lore confirmation. The episode does not initiate the overarching main story.
