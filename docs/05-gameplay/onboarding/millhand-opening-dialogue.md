**Status: PROPOSED dialogue, for user review (2026-10-14).** Guther Anvil, the Smithing Foreman; Millhand's background Craftsmanship +3; the workshop hub; the Missing Stamp twist; and the global tutorial structure/economy are already LOCKED. **These exact lines and player responses are not locked yet.** No new named NPCs.

## Scene M01 — At the Smithing workshop

**Trigger:** first player entry into Millhand's regular Smithing workshop after character creation. This is the **opening conversation only**, before the ordinary Smithing work and before the unique *Missing Stamp* discovery. Later return visits should use a regular repeatable job menu instead of replaying this scene.

**Ambient narration (brief, not a cutscene):**

> The workshop smells of wet coal and hot iron. Somewhere beyond the furnaces, a hammer keeps a miserable, stubborn rhythm. Guther Anvil is holding a municipal work docket under a lamp, trying to make its crooked figures behave.

**Guther Anvil:**

> "Shut that door. We're heating iron here, not the whole bloody city."
>
> "Council wants another batch of worker plates. Numbers straight, stamps clean, no clever shortcuts. You've done this kind of work before."

**Player may ask any of these optional questions (no skill roll, no reward/relationship changes):**

**Option A — "Which plates?"**

> **Guther:** "Top of the docket. Match the number to the blank, seat the die square, then strike. A wrong digit and the whole lot goes back across the counter."

**Option B — "What if something doesn't match?"**

> **Guther:** "Then you put the hammer down and show me. You don't mend ink with iron. I've seen men try."

**Option C — "When do we get paid?"**

> **Guther:** "When their inspector accepts the lot. Not before. City men can hold a purse tighter than a rivet."

**Converging line (whether questions were used or skipped):**

> **Guther:** "Docket on the bench. Tools are there. Check the blank, line the stamp up, and watch your fingers. I need plates, not a handful of you."

**Required interaction:** **[Begin platework]** → launches **ordinary reusable Smithing task**: inspect identification number and plate, prepare/select proper method, align die, resolve work complication and submit plate. Do not fire the Missing Stamp choice yet.

## Mechanics / integrity notes

- The character **knows Guther and their trade**. No first-meeting tutorial exposition, memory loss or invented date/time of day.
- The opening dialogue does **not** disclose the fatal-workshop-fire number or fresh inspection mark. The **Missing Stamp incident happens after the ordinary work interactions**.
- Guther's municipal-inspection condition establishes why wages can be withheld once the lot is disputed. The **actual scripted tutorial reward is 0 coins, 0 ordinary XP**, with no post-interaction surprise payout. In any task UI, indicate **“Payment subject to council acceptance”** rather than inventing a guaranteed wage that will later be taken away. Ordinary Smithing jobs after onboarding continue to pay coins and XP.
- The background already grants **Craftsmanship +3 at creation**. The **+1 different Major Skill level** occurs only when committing to one of the later three *Missing Stamp* follow-up tasks; not during this opening conversation. No other bonus.
- Optional questions are **flavour/information choices**, not permanent story branches or disguised skill checks. They rejoin at the same work interaction.
- No combat, clinic, rent or second-task dialogue appears in the first conversation. Those steps remain further into the E2E.
- Use concise text panels; avoid a heavy cutscene that delays learning the ordinary Smithing mechanics.
