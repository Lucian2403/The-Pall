# Millhand — Stage 7: Clinic editorial review

Status: Draft, pending approval, 2026-10-23.

The approved shared clinic mechanics and combat log are unchanged. This writing review covers medical scene dialogue and the personal Journal only.

## Scene 7A — Arrival and examination

**After a Flint victory:** *You leave the passage and reach the clinic.*

**After a Flint defeat:** *Another worker helps you reach the clinic.*

*There is a place to sit. A clinician is available to examine you.*

**Instruction:** Check your condition and speak to the clinician.

**Player action: Examine condition.**

*You check your Health and any conditions recorded after the fight.*

**Service panel:** Actual Health and injuries (or None), Examination and basic care if needed, **10 coins**. No injury, healing or bonus is manufactured by this panel.

**Player action: Speak to the clinician.**

**CLINICIAN:** “Sit. Let me see.”

*The clinician examines you.*

### No recorded injury

**EXAMINATION RESULT:** No injuries found. No treatment needed.

**PLAYER:** “How much?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins.”

**PLAYER:** “Ten?”

**CLINICIAN (new proposed line):** “For the examination.”

The unhurt character receives no dressing, treatment or Health adjustment. This new line should be shared with other origin clinics only after owner approval.

### Actual cut or scrape requiring a dressing

**CLINICIAN (existing approved line):** “Needs cleaning. I'll dress it.”

**PLAYER:** “How much?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins.”

**PLAYER:** “Ten?”

**CLINICIAN:** “That includes the dressing.”

Show actual injury and appropriate care; never reuse these words for an unhurt character or a different wound.

### Other actual condition

Display the diagnosed condition and available service using the shared medical system, without inventing wound details or treatment.

**PLAYER:** “How much?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins.”

**PLAYER:** “Ten?”

**CLINICIAN (new proposed general line):** “For the examination and any care you need.”

Do not promise that the condition will be cured unless real mechanics support it.
