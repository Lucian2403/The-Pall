# Millhand — Stage 7: The Clinic — Editorial Revision 01

Status: Draft, pending approval, 2026-10-23.

The approved shared clinic mechanics and combat log are unchanged. This writing review covers medical scene dialogue and the personal Journal only.

## Scene 7A — Arrival and examination

**After a Flint victory:** *You leave the passage and reach the clinic.*

**After a Flint defeat:** *Another worker helps you reach the clinic.*

*There is a place to sit. A clinician is available to examine you.*

**Instruction:** Check your condition and speak to the clinician.

**Player action: Examine condition.**

*You check your Health and any conditions recorded after the fight.*

**Service panel:** Actual Health and injuries (or None), Examination and basic care if needed, **10 coins**. No injury, healing or bonus is manufactured by this panel.

**Player action: Speak to the clinician.**

**CLINICIAN:** “Sit. Let me see.”

*The clinician examines you.*

### No recorded injury

**EXAMINATION RESULT:** No injuries found. No treatment needed.

**PLAYER:** “How much?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins.”

**PLAYER:** “Ten?”

**CLINICIAN (new proposed line):** “For the examination.”

The unhurt character receives no dressing, treatment or Health adjustment. This new line should be shared with other origin clinics only after owner approval.

### Actual cut or scrape requiring a dressing

**CLINICIAN (existing approved line):** “Needs cleaning. I'll dress it.”

**PLAYER:** “How much?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins.”

**PLAYER:** “Ten?”

**CLINICIAN:** “That includes the dressing.”

Show actual injury and appropriate care; never reuse these words for an unhurt character or a different wound.

### Other actual condition

Display the diagnosed condition and available service using the shared medical system, without inventing wound details or treatment.

**PLAYER:** “How much?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins.”

**PLAYER:** “Ten?”

**CLINICIAN (new proposed general line):** “For the examination and any care you need.”

Do not promise that the condition will be cured unless real mechanics support it.

## Scene 7B — Payment and care

**Current instruction:** Review the fee before agreeing.

**Service:** Examination, with basic care only where needed.
**Fee:** 10 coins.
**Coins before paying:** 20.

**Player action: Pay 10 coins.**

*You pay ten coins. The clinician completes the examination and any basic care shown before payment.*

**Unhurt result:** No treatment is given. Your Health and condition are unchanged.

**Injured result:** The clinician performs only the care supported by the actual medical system. Show the real Health and condition changes; do not claim full recovery without a supported result.

**Coins remaining:** 10.

The service must charge **exactly once**. After a reload, double click or revisit, show the completed treatment rather than a second payment. No item, normal XP, medical skill level or hidden charge is awarded.

## Scene 7C — Leave the clinic

**Player action: Leave the clinic.**

*You leave the clinic after the examination. Your current condition remains the result recorded by the medical service.*

Continue toward the same starter rented home through normal city travel. No rent notice is shown here.

## Read-only interactions

- Check condition: show actual saved Health and conditions.
- Review cost: show 10 coins without charging.
- Attempt unnecessary dressing while unhurt: do not offer it.
- Attempt to leave before required examination: “You haven't completed the examination yet.”
- Repeat payment: show the completed service, without charging again.
- Open Journal: ordinary navigation, not a medical action.

## Journal — personal first-person account

**After arrival:**

*Made it to the clinic after the fight with Flint. I've not got much money left, but I ought to get checked.*

**After paying for an examination that finds no injury:**

*They couldn't find anything wrong with me. Good. Still cost ten coins just to be examined. Half my money gone, and I haven't earned a thing today.*

**After actual care for a treatable injury:**

*The clinician checked me over and treated what they could. Ten coins for the examination and care. I needed the help, but that's half my money gone. Still nothing paid for the council work.*

If the health system records a condition but provides no treatment, write an honest variant based on that result; do not falsely claim care was given.

**Optional final note:**

*I've got ten coins left. Time to head home.*

## Approved mechanics unchanged

Examination follows victory or defeat, even when unhurt. It costs 10 coins (20 → 10) and grants no XP, loot or level. Treatment depends on genuine recorded injuries. The combat log stays unchanged. Stage 8 begins only after leaving and returning home. Dialogue and Journal wording in this document await approval.
