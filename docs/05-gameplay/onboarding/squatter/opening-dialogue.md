# Squatter — Stage 1: Benny Ben at Hedge Shelter (PROPOSED)

**Status: PROPOSED FOR USER APPROVAL (2026-10-15); NOT LOCKED.** The [Squatter master](README.md) already locks **Hedge Shelter**, **Benny Ben — Herb Dealer**, the ordinary **Fieldcraft / Foraging** first job *Yarrow Cut*, and the later *Warm Patch* incident. This document proposes only the initial spoken exchange and handoff into gathering. No future discovery is revealed in Stage 1.

## Scene S01 — A routine herb order

*At the Hedge Shelter, Benny Ben brushes loose dirt off the weighing scale. A few bundles of yarrow are set to one side.*

**BENNY:** “I've got a yarrow order to fill. There should be enough along the marked path.”

**PLAYER (optional):** “Do you want the flowers, or the whole stems?”

**BENNY (optional reply):** “Cut the good stems and leave the roots. Don't strip the patch bare.”

**BENNY (convergence):** “The amount's on the board. Bring it back here when you're done, and we'll sort it.”

**[Check the order board]** → inspect the assigned Yarrow quantity, nearby marked public patch and ordinary Foraging job details. **[Take the job]** → begin *Yarrow Cut* through normal plant identification, careful harvesting and return-to-scale interactions.

## Intent and boundaries

Benny talks like someone who buys and sorts plants for a living, not a tutorial narrator. His concern is usable stock and keeping a working patch alive. The player already knows how to forage, but asking what portion of the plant is wanted is an ordinary job question. If the optional question is skipped, Benny still gives enough information to proceed. Do not turn him into a mystery informant or imply he already knows about the refuse and remains by the later outfall.

Keep the work board in the scene, and put exact harvest quantities, sorting measures and timer details in the normal job interface rather than in Benny's speech. No numeric target or new harvesting skill gate is proposed here. The *Warm Patch* discovery waits until after the normal first-job interactions, as previously locked.

Stage 2 will define the reusable gathering work; Stage 3 will reveal the already approved *Warm Patch* incident. Both remain separate user approvals.
