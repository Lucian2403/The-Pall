# Squatter — Stage 4: Three Second-Skill Tasks (PROPOSED)

**Status: PROPOSED FOR USER APPROVAL (2026-10-15); NOT LOCKED.** Stage 1 [Benny](opening-dialogue.md), Stage 2 [Yarrow Cut](yarrow-cut-interaction.md), and Stage 3 [The Warm Patch](warm-patch-discovery.md) are APPROVED. This draft covers **one** route selected at Stage 3; exact dialogue, work choices and site-specific handling need approval. The three job families and the core discovery in each are already locked in the [Squatter overview](README.md).

The player has seen dressings caught in the yarrow, a human finger bone and warm drainage bricks by the old poorhouse ground. Those observations **raise a question**, not a medical diagnosis or an established supernatural explanation. Benny's order is weighed but its **full buyer acceptance remains disputed**. Staff in each branch know only what the player tells them or what they inspect themselves.

## A — The Apothecary's Weighing (Commerce → Trading)

**Entry:** The Squatter chose **[Take the order to the apothecary without mentioning the patch]**. The job-marked consignment, previously weighed at Benny's scale, comes to the working apothecary's receiving counter. The buyer is unnamed and has no reason to know the player's private discovery.

**Activity, using normal Trading receipt and sorting systems:**

1. **[Check the delivery against the weight note]** — compare Benny's recorded usable weight to the delivery, with damaged or damp bundles plainly visible; fix an accounting mismatch if necessary. No hidden value puzzle.
2. **[Sort for the buyer's inspection]**, choosing **[Separate doubtful stems first]** (more checking time, clearer separation) or **[Weigh each marked group]** (more repeated handling, clearer per-group weight). This is a real quantity/condition trading task, not a dialogue quiz.
3. **[Compare the accepted and held weights]** — buyer inspects the separated groups and checks source records. A **confirmed clean portion** from the demonstrably separate, dry upper public ground may be taken into the buyer's ordinary inventory. Its separation must be grounded in source notes and normal inspection; healthy appearance alone does **not** prove uncontaminated medicine. The buyer holds/rejects the suspicious group and **does not sell it as medicinal stock**. Exact quality proof and accepted weight follow the general system; the tutorial needs a distinguishable safe subsection regardless of Stage 2 harvesting method.
4. **[Record the partial receipt]** — the buyer records only what was accepted. This is not approval of the full disputed yarrow order.

*The apothecary rubs gray residue from a tied stem against a scrap of paper.*

**APOTHECARY:** “Where did Benny get this lot?”

**PLAYER:** “From the yarrow patch along the marked path. Why?”

**APOTHECARY:** “I've seen dirt like this on bundles brought up from the old poorhouse drain.”

**PLAYER:** “Are you saying the herbs are bad?”

**APOTHECARY:** “I'm saying I'm not taking these ones. I'll buy what we can check properly. People still need medicine.”

**Player choice:** **[Ask what will happen to the held bundle]** or **[Check the accepted weight]**. The first gets “It stays here, off the shelves, till we know where it came from.” The second opens the receipt with accepted and rejected weights. Both lead to the same concrete finding.

**Revelation:** The apothecary recognizes the disposal-ground residue but pragmatically buys the **verifiably clean portion**, while refusing the doubtful stems. He knows there's a risk and chooses a limited transaction, not the sale of unsafe medicine.

**Payment clarity:** the partial receipt settles only the portion credited to **Benny's consignment account**. It is not a wage receipt to the player or full acceptance of the original Yarrow Cut assignment. That full-batch order remains unresolved, hence no tutorial worker payment clears. Do not imply the player received the buyer's coins and then had them taken away; no cash goes into the character's inventory.

**End:** The player has an accurate trade record and knows that a buyer has seen similar marks before, but cannot name the source of the drain or claim every yarrow plant is tainted. The rejected goods stay with the apothecary, under hold, not as player loot.

## B — Suspect Cuttings (Medicine → Herbalism)

**Entry:** The Squatter chose **[Warn the infirmary about the yarrow patch]**. They report what they saw to an unnamed **infirmary herbalist**. This person handles herbs at a staffed infirmary and is not the later combat-treatment clinician. The weighed order stays with Benny pending examination unless the player has explicitly told him and he has set it aside; either way it is **not supplied to a patient**.

**Activity, using ordinary supervised Herbalism:**

1. **[Describe the source and compare labels]** — show where the questionable yarrow came from against infirmary stock labels. Visible source records distinguish a reserve known to come from an ordinary clean garden from the newly reported doubtful patch. No diagnosis is made from a visual stain.
2. **[Sort the preparation stock]** — select **[Set aside the doubtful yarrow]** first, or **[Check the clean reserve labels]** first. Both are legitimate approaches, different in sequence/time, but neither mixes unverified stems into medicinal stock.
3. **[Prepare confirmed clean cuttings]** with the herbalist: ordinary sorting/drying/bundling/appropriate handling as allowed by the common Herbalism system. The supervised worker provides site tools. No attempt to “wash away” unproven contamination or invented treatment recipe.
4. **[Enter the source concern in the work notes]** — keep suspect ground out of use, note where the dressings and outfall were seen. Do not falsely declare a specific disease.

**Opening exchange:**

**HERBALIST:** “Benny sent you with herbs?”

**PLAYER:** “Not exactly. I found old bandages caught in the yarrow near the poorhouse drain. There was a bone in the ground too.”

**HERBALIST:** “Don't bring any of that yarrow in here. Show me where it came from.”

**PLAYER:** “I haven't brought it. I just wanted someone to know.”

**HERBALIST:** “Good. Help me check what we've already got.”

**Discovery exchange after comparing stock notes:**

**PLAYER:** “Could those bandages have come from here?”

**HERBALIST:** “We used to send old dressings down by that outfall. I thought they'd stopped doing it.”

**PLAYER:** “So the rubbish could be washing back into the herb patch?”

**HERBALIST:** “It could. I'll put the patch in the notes. No one should use those cuttings until it's looked at.”

**Revelation:** The infirmary's old disposal arrangement may have fed refuse into the same ground where medicinal plants grow. Staff knew of the former disposal route but did not know—or admit to knowing—that it affected the herb patch. This exchange **does not prove** the exposed bone came from the infirmary or fix the exact cause of drainage warmth.

**End:** The herbalist sets a **source hold** on the suspect bed and records the report; verified reserve herbs remain usable. The player's supervised assistance is part of the immediate response to the warning, not an agreed paid staff shift.

## C — Clear the Outfall (Industry → Construction)

**Entry:** The Squatter chose **[Follow the warm drainage channel]**. They ask the unnamed **ditch-crew lead** working on an outer public section to check the partly collapsed channel. The player does **not** enter the drainage tunnel, remove remains, climb into contaminated water or perform an unsafe repair alone.

**Activity, using standard basic Construction support:**

1. **[Inspect the outer support and blockage]** — compare intact brickwork with the weakened outer section from the dry public side. The crew marks where they can work safely; a real structure check rather than another paperwork test.
2. **[Choose how to help]**: **[Hold the support while the crew checks the edge]** (slower but keeps their measurement steady) or **[Clear loose debris from the outer grille]** using crew-supplied ordinary tools (more physical handling, leaves the interior untouched). Both supervised, low-tier job actions; no free personal equipment.
3. **[Check what the freed edge exposed]** — as workers shift loose rubbish at the *outer* grille, old **pauper-welfare tags and medical linen** are seen trapped together in the debris. The crew records the finding without treating remains as salvage, collecting a sellable component or claiming a newly revealed secret official culprit.
4. **[Mark the problem for the crew]** — note the obstructed channel and disturbed poorhouse material for later authorized work. The player does not complete an imaginary full drain restoration or solve the source of its warmth.

**Opening exchange:**

**PLAYER:** “Could you have a look at the old outfall? It's warm by the yarrow beds, and the ground's coming away.”

**DITCH LEAD:** “We can check the outside. Keep off those loose bricks.”

**PLAYER:** “Is that drain still in use?”

**DITCH LEAD:** “Water still runs through it. That's all I can tell you till we get it open.”

**Discovery exchange:**

*An old welfare tag slips from the wet debris beside a strip of medical linen.*

**PLAYER:** “That's a poorhouse tag, isn't it? What's it doing in the drain?”

**DITCH LEAD:** “Looks like one. There's old dressings in there too.”

**PLAYER:** “And all that's running under the yarrow?”

**DITCH LEAD:** “Same channel. Don't go digging at it. I'll put it in the work book.”

**Revelation:** The channel runs beneath the nearby herb ground and carries discarded material tied to the poorhouse, including welfare tags and medical linen. Its neglected condition threatens local water and herbs. Neither the identity of the buried person nor the physical cause of the warmth has been solved.

**End:** The crew secures its immediate outside work area and records the unsafe outfall; the blocked or degraded **inner** channel still needs authorized repair. The player helped with ordinary crew work in response to their own discovery, not a separately negotiated commission.

## Convergence to Stage 5 — The Rustling Bank

After completing exactly one chosen work session, the player takes the **ordinary public approach along the outer drainage bank toward the city gate**. Each service point connects to that approach; do not teleport the character or presume any particular unapproved street geometry. Only Stage 4's selected branch finding is saved as their local story knowledge.

The original yarrow order's full acceptance stays unresolved. In Trading a partial clean consignment may be credited to Benny; in Herbalism or Construction the reported source risk prevents the original medicinal order being cleared. None of this means Benny knowingly sold unsafe medicine. Exact prices, quantity thresholds, medicine safety checks, drain engineering and time effects are future common-system work.

Stage 5's animal has not seen the player's clinic conversation, trade receipt or ditch work; the same frightened wild boar can plausibly be at the edge of the disturbed bank whichever job was chosen. **The boar's injury cause is unknown**.
