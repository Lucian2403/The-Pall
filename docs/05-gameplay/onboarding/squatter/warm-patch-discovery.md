# Squatter — Stage 3: The Warm Patch (APPROVED)

**Status: APPROVED AND LOCKED (2026-10-15).** The premise, discoveries, three choices and second Major Skill routes were already agreed in the [Squatter master](README.md). The user approved the playable discovery, spoken player reactions, first-order acceptance dispute and immediate route handoffs. Stage 1 is [approved](opening-dialogue.md); Stage 2 is [approved](yarrow-cut-interaction.md). Stage 4 will script the different follow-up jobs separately.

## What the player discovers

The Squatter has finished the regular Yarrow Cut work: identifying the plant, choosing a harvest approach, sorting stems and having the bundle weighed at Benny's Hedge Shelter. The weighed order is in Benny's custody pending the intended buyer's acceptance, not a permanent herb item in the player's bag.

The player heads toward the city gate on the same familiar outskirts path. On passing the yarrow bed again, a disturbed corner beside the drainage bricks catches their eye. Recent rain has washed loose soil away. **This public-side view** was not visible from the area used for the ordinary cut, and the task now stops being routine.

**Scene direction:**

*The yarrow grows thick beside a sagging brick outfall. Water runs under the roots. Old strips of dressing cloth have caught on the stems where the rain washed soil away. A small human finger bone shows at the edge of the ground.*

*The bricks are faintly warm to the touch on their accessible, dry outer edge. Beyond them lies the old poorhouse disposal ground.*

Do not make the player crawl into the outfall, handle unknown waste, taste the herbs, remove a human bone or step into contaminated water. The scene is in the **protected outskirts**, not a Pall expedition or entry into a danger zone.

## S03-A — Look closely enough to understand the risk

The player can inspect the three visible clues in any order. The game does not require a high Investigation or Medicine skill.

1. **[Look at the dressing cloth]** — ordinary-looking remnants of used medical dressings are tangled in the yarrow. The player can recognize a dressing without diagnosing what the cloth carries. The precise origin of each piece is unknown.
2. **[Look at the exposed bone]** — a small human finger bone lies in the disturbed edge of the soil. The old poorhouse disposal ground is nearby; **who this person was and how their remains came to be here are not known**.
3. **[Check the drainage bricks]** — the outfall is partly collapsed, runs beside/under the bed and gives off a faint warmth. There is no reason yet to claim a supernatural cause, active Pall contamination or a fixed disease hazard.

**Approved player reactions (spoken as the evidence is observed, not a monologue):**

**PLAYER (on the cloth):** “Those look like old bandages. Right where I was picking the yarrow.”

**PLAYER (on the bone):** “Is that a finger bone?”

The discoveries stand by themselves. Benny is not magically present and does not suddenly know about the poorhouse's remains. No one is named, arrested or accused.

After enough is seen to recognize that the **medicinal harvest may have come from unsafe ground**, the player can review the three clues again or make the consequential choice. The key is uncertainty: the yarrow may look healthy, but appearance does not prove that a medicine crop is safe.

## S03-B — What happens to the original order?

The ordinary first-job report remains marked **buyer acceptance pending**. Benny's scale confirmed the quantity, not the medicinal safety of its source, and no customer has accepted the entire order as clean medicinal stock. This explains why a foraging worker could do their part and still be left without a cleared payment. It does **not** make Benny a fraudster who intentionally withheld money.

The initial order cannot be signed off as a full accepted medicinal batch while the source is in doubt. The exact acceptance shortfall/quality record will be evidenced in Stage 4: in the trading route the apothecary checks and buys only a verified clean portion; in the other routes the unresolved contamination concern prevents the order from clearing as delivered. This is the **approved causal resolution for this exceptional tutorial job**, not a rule that normal Foraging jobs are unpaid or that a small amount of dirt invalidates every harvest.

**Do not script Benny claiming to know about the contaminated soil when the player chose not to tell him.** The buyer's acceptance is pending for ordinary business reasons before the discovery; the suspicious source gives the player an ethical reason to investigate or warn others. No false advance payment is made in Stage 2.

## S03-C — Choose what matters now

The following three **route meanings and job families are already locked**. The visible choices and short optional lines are approved. All options appear without skill, item or moral-point prerequisites.

### A — [Take the order to the apothecary without mentioning the patch]

**Commerce → Trading — The Apothecary's Weighing**

The player makes the normal onward delivery of Benny's already weighed consignment, avoiding mention of the dressing strips, bone and warm outfall. Benny still does not know about the discovery. The player collects only the **job-marked consignment** as a temporary delivery prop, not tradeable inventory.

**PLAYER (short aside, optional):** “I don't even know what's happened out here. The apothecary can check the cuttings.”

The player is not declared evil or stupid for uncertainty. The choice does, however, mean that the player **has not warned the buyer about what they saw**. Stage 4's ordinary trading/inspection interaction reveals that the apothecary recognizes stains on some bundles and buys only the verified clean portion. Do not grant the player a complete sale merely for staying silent, or imply that the buyer knowingly sells contaminated stock.

**Immediate next destination:** the working apothecary's weighing/receiving counter. Benny remains at Hedge Shelter.

### B — [Warn the infirmary about the yarrow patch]

**Medicine → Herbalism — Suspect Cuttings**

The player decides someone preparing medicine should know where the cuttings grew. They go to the established infirmary/clinic herbal-work area and describe the dressing cloth, exposed bone and outfall; they **do not** medically diagnose contamination. The harvest stays under Benny's pending order rather than being sold as safe stock. A supervised herbalist helps check suspicious versus usable plants through the ordinary Herbalism system.

**PLAYER (short aside, optional):** “Those herbs could end up in someone's dressing. I need to tell the infirmary.”

**Immediate next destination:** the staff herbal-work area. The staff cannot be presumed guilty merely because the discarded dressings appear to be medical.

### C — [Follow the warm drainage channel]

**Industry → Construction — Clear the Outfall**

The player decides the source of the water and warmth needs examining before anyone uses the ground. From the safe public edge, they seek an existing local ditch/works crew, rather than crawling into the old brick tunnel alone. Construction staff supervise simple structural/clearing support through reusable work interactions.

**PLAYER (short aside, optional):** “That drain runs right under the plants. I'd better find someone who can look at it.”

**Immediate next destination:** a public outfall access point with the ditch crew. Stage 4 reveals welfare tags and medical linens, but does not identify the buried person or explain the warmth as a new supernatural force.

## S03-D — Optional Benny exchange (only after choosing a warning/investigation route)

Benny's user-approved character cares about both the crop and what lies beyond it. He **can** be told of the discovery if the player returns to Hedge Shelter; the optional conversation is not a mandatory stop while there may be a contamination risk. This response should not appear on the silent trading path unless the player *separately chooses to disclose the find* in a future authored interaction.

*The player finds Benny sorting stems at the scale.*

**PLAYER:** “Benny, I went past the patch again. There's old dressing cloth in the plants. And I saw a bone in the ground.”

**BENNY:** “A bone? Right beside the yarrow?”

**PLAYER:** “Near the old outfall. I don't know how long it's been there.”

**BENNY:** “Don't let anyone pick from that edge. I'll keep these cuttings aside till somebody has a proper look.”

Benny reacts first to the **specific plants and unsafe edge**, not with a sermon about how the city treats nature. He cannot determine what contaminated the herbs or who was buried. This optional exchange changes his knowledge and the disposition of the still-pending stock, not the selected Major Skill or the player's Stage 4 destination. If the player skips it on the Medicine/Industry route, staff can still find out through the player's own report; Benny may remain unaware until a natural later conversation.
