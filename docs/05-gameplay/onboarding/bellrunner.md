# Bellrunner — Proposed Starter Location and First Job

**Status: PROPOSAL, for user review (2026-10-13).** Do not treat names, job details or inventory as locked without approval. The background name, role and **Industry +3 Major Skill** at character creation are already agreed in [Character Creation](../../01-player/character-creation.md).

## Locked common character-creation context

- Bellrunner is a Grand Cylinder City messenger / shift courier background.
- Six backgrounds total, each with a male and female fixed portrait; Bellrunner grants **Industry +3** and no attribute increase.
- Origin introduction is a compact **self-contained E2E episode**, not the beginning of the overarching main story. The episode eventually ends with rent coming due.
- Combat throughout the game is **turn-based**; combat implementation is postponed. Bellrunner is **not yet committed** to exactly the same fight or length as Millhand.

## Proposed beginning — location and recurring NPC

- **Starter location: The Dispatch House.** Noisy city parcel-and-shift-notice sorting hall: duty bells, ink-stained route board, wooden freight counter, coal-smudged couriers and carts.
- **Persistent gameplay role:** generic hub for **Industry / Hauling courier and light-delivery jobs** throughout the game, not a one-use tutorial set.
- **Recurring NPC: Dispatch Master** (title only, personal name TBD), assigns routine deliveries and pays **ordinary Industry job coins and experience** via reusable job resolutions.
- **First action:** speak to Dispatch Master, receive a modest route bundle and inspect the job briefing. Character starts in a familiar workplace; player learns the UI and world through work rather than exposition.

## Proposed ordinary job — Shift Dispatch

The player is assigned a short, paid courier circuit carrying municipal shift notices. This belongs to **Industry / Hauling**, not a unique Bellrunner-only job system.

Generic job interactions to reuse in future courier/Hauling assignments:
1. **Accept/prepare:** inspect docket, delivery count, carrying weight, duration and expected pay.
2. **Route selection:** choose between a shorter busy lane and a longer easier route (real time / inconvenience tradeoff).
3. **Delivery discrepancy:** use a reusable address verification, damaged docket, queue management or receiver-confirmation interaction.
4. **Regular resolution:** apply ordinary currency / Industry XP and any generic time/condition effects.

### Dark opening incident — one possible hook, not yet approved

**Working title: The Wrong Bell.** Among routine messages is a sealed order to start a night shift at a block officially closed for unsafe fumes. The shift bell still rings, and a watchman expects workers to enter. The dispatch docket gives a perfectly ordinary signature requirement. A choice here could branch into a different Major Skill job, as in Millhand, but **the hook, options, exact follow-on jobs, fight/healing requirements and ending are entirely TBD**.

This should feel like grim labor governance and human risk, **not a repeat of Millhand's false death-register stamp plot**.
