## S07-A — Reach ordinary medical care

Owler has left the night door and the adult patient is now safe in normal ward care: the unnecessary demonstration is cancelled **for this patient alone**. Owler's result remains **player victory or player defeat**, not a different patient outcome.

- **Victory:** the Stitcher walks from the night door to the infirmary's normal treatment area through ordinary movement. An injury may or may not actually exist.
- **Defeat:** an unnamed ward worker helps the Stitcher into the same ordinary treatment area after the player is knocked down. This does **not** erase the combat loss.
- No transfer is resumed while the player is receiving care; the already witnessed refusal and Claudia's decision have closed this case. Do not give the player a new rescue task before the clinic.

Use the **existing care provider role**, e.g. **CLINICIAN** (unnamed), at Cinder Row Infirmary. It is a regular hospital service, **not Claudia's personal treatment minigame or a separate Stitcher-only clinic**. The medical staff member is not secretly involved in the teaching contract and doesn't need an invented biography.

## S07-B — Approved dialogue

*An ordinary clinician points toward an examination seat.*

**CLINICIAN:** “Sit down. What happened?”

**PLAYER:** “Owler tried to take a patient out. We had a fight.”

**CLINICIAN:** “Let's have a look at you.”

**[Examine condition]** — open the normal Care / Treatment screen displaying the character's **actual** current Health, known injuries and available care. No visible wound is assumed from the word “fight” or from a player victory. Do not select a dressing or injury treatment before checking the player's condition.

*The clinician finishes checking the player and displays the fee and any appropriate care.*

**PLAYER:** “I have to pay for this?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins.”

**PLAYER:** “I work here.”

**CLINICIAN:** “I know. Ten coins.”

**[Pay 10 coins and accept care]** — charge once, provide only medically appropriate care according to the common Health system. The small exchange underscores the institution's priorities without inventing a new reason why the patient must pay or making the clinician sound like a villain. Claudia need not appear to make another speech.

**[Leave the infirmary]** — ordinary travel resumes toward the shared rented home. No rent note or closing speech triggers inside the infirmary.

## S07-C — Ordinary shared service flow

1. Arrival uses the **actual Owler outcome**; record it separately from the medical result.
2. **[Examine condition]**: show Health, actual wounds/conditions if any, expected service outcome and **10 coins** as the fixed tutorial charge. This is the common provider workflow, not a special Medicine +3 player action.
3. **[Speak to clinician]**: exchange above. In particular, nothing in these lines asserts an injury that might not exist. If the common UI later shows a particular wound type, let the service panel provide appropriate treatment wording rather than writing a false generic “I'll dress it.”
4. **[Pay 10 coins and accept care]**: with the established zero tutorial wages/XP/loot and no other spending, coins go from **20 to 10**. The actual Health restoration, wound effects, time cost and potential diagnosis remain global-system TBD. Do not award Medicine XP because the Stitcher has received care.
5. **[Leave the infirmary]**: Stage 7 completes once and hands off to the **identical [shared Stage 8](../shared-home-rent-ending.md)**. No new mandatory job or institution-related side quest interrupts the home transition.
