## S03-A — A bed that isn't on the round

The ordinary *Morning Dressings* job has finished, its supplies have been recorded, and Claudia has signed off the charity-bed cover round. Its **0-coin, 0-ordinary-XP** status was disclosed at the start as an unpaid charity cover. The player has not seen anything about surgical teaching during this work.

*Claudia enters a side ward to shut it for the day. A man lies awake on a narrow bed. Straps lie across his wrists. Blue ink marks straight lines along the skin near an old wound. He is conscious and watching the doorway.*

He is an **adult**, not a child. There is no depiction of surgery, cutting or explicit injury. The infirmary's ordinary background noise continues outside. The Matron is physically present, not magically appearing in response to a clue.

**[Look at the patient]** — he is awake, in restraints, and can speak for himself. The player does not need to examine or touch the ink lines, release the straps immediately, or assume he is guilty of anything.

**[Read the bedside chart]** — the line normally used to justify the procedure contains no necessary treatment for his old wound. Instead its conspicuous payment/transfer entry reads **exactly**:

> **CHARITY ACCOUNT SETTLED — TEACHING DEMONSTRATION, DAWN.**

**[Ask the patient what happened]** — allowed before or after the chart inspection, without a skill test:

**PATIENT:** “They let me sleep. That's when they took my thumbprint.”

The claim is his testimony at this stage, **not yet a verified record of sedation**. His exact name and medical history remain unknown. Do not invent a named patient or pretend a thumbprint on a paper conclusively establishes informed consent.

### Optional short player response

**PLAYER:** “You didn't agree to it?”

**PATIENT:** “Not to that. They said they'd see to my wound.”

He has received some care; the chart's claimed teaching operation is unnecessary. His inability to pay is being converted into a fee in the form of his body and future suffering. Do not allege that all city hospitals make these arrangements.

## S03-B — Ask Claudia

*Claudia closes the casebook on the end table. She looks at the chart rather than the man.*

**PLAYER:** “What's the demonstration for? He says he never agreed to it.”

**CLAUDIA:** “The school pays for the ward. He had his care.”

**PLAYER:** “That doesn't mean they can do what they like with him.”

**CLAUDIA:** “Keep him, and tonight the fever ward has no heat.”

**PLAYER:** “He can't even get out of the bed.”

**CLAUDIA:** “Then don't stand there arguing. I've got a ward to keep open.”

This uses the previously locked Claudia quotation, but gives it a personal cost: the Matron is defending a financial arrangement that keeps vulnerable patients supplied at the expense of **this one patient's consent**. Her manner is cold, practical and contemptuous of interference. She need not smile at anybody in this scene—her beauty and ability to charm do not make her less culpable.

The Matron **does not** admit to forging the thumbprint or personally signing for a sedated patient; that specific proof belongs to Stage 4's Contracts route. She knows there is a teaching arrangement, knows it is not needed treatment, and refuses to set aside the ward funding simply because the patient objects. She is not physically attacking the player, and Owler is not introduced until the later service-door encounter.

If the player skips the optional second patient question, the chart and his locked thumbprint line still provide enough information. Claudia's exchange stays short; do not force a ten-question dialogue tree.

## S03-C — Three decisions, all viable

After seeing the chart and hearing the patient (in any order), present three choices. They describe **how to act**, not a Good/Evil morality meter or three difficulty modes.

| Player decision | Immediate intent | Stage 4 job, distinct Major Skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Follow the transfer]** | Learn when and where the patient will be moved; stay close enough to intervene | **Industry → Hauling — The Night Trolley** |
| **[Contest the consent paper]** | Establish whether the ward actually has lawful authority to transfer him | **Commerce → Contracts — The Signed Consent** |
| **[Examine the surgeon's notes]** | Check whether any medical treatment actually requires this operation | **Scholarship → Investigation — Notes for the Living** |

**Exact short spoken player lines (proposed):**

A. **PLAYER:** “I'll go with the trolley. I want to see where they're taking him.”

B. **PLAYER:** “Show me the paper they say he signed.”

C. **PLAYER:** “I want to see what the surgeon wrote.”

Claudia need not give an approving speech to any choice. Her terse convergence:

**CLAUDIA:** “Do as you please. Just stay out of my way.”

The player's selected action should open the **real ordinary work template** for that discipline in Stage 4. Any involved ward paperwork, keys and carts are facility/job props, not new free inventory.

### Progression at the choice, once

Immediately when the player commits to **one** route, grant exactly **+1 level in Industry, Commerce or Scholarship Major Skill** according to the chosen route. This is independent of the initial Medicine +3 created at character creation. No bonus for inspecting extra clues; one-time flags prevent repeated choice/reload rewards. Stage 4's exceptional task pays **0 coins and 0 ordinary XP**, as does the first task. Normal paid recurring jobs remain paid.

This second task is unpaid because the player is taking part in an immediate ward transfer / review / inquiry during an already unpaid charity cover—**not a newly commissioned paid shift**. Make that distinction visible in its job details. The institution has not promised wages for secretly moving this patient or investigating the disputed arrangement.
