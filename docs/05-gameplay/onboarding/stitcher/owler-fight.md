## S06-A — What the fight is actually about

Owler is a worn-out hospital porter **paid by the anatomy school to complete this one transfer**. He has a transfer paper but no uncontested handover: the patient has **refused aloud before a ward witness**, and the trolley is still inside the infirmary behind an attendant. Claudia has ordered a temporary stop, partly to protect herself and the infirmary from an obvious dispute.

Owler tries to shove his way through to retrieve the trolley or enforce the old order. The player's goal is to **keep him from forcing the transfer**. This is an ordinary, short, real **turn-based PvE** fight against a tired but capable man, not a duel with a surgeon, police force or supernatural creature.

**The player starts unarmed** with Basic Clothes, a Crude Small Bag, 20 coins and Medicine +3 with one different Major Skill +1. No free clinical weapon, injectable drug, new weapon proficiency, bonus XP or loot. Owler's possible short truncheon/leather strap is an existing **TBD choice**, not yet a fixed combat item.

These are scene-level facts only. The shared combat system must assign defensible actual positions/turn effects later; no invented fixed defence percentages or automatic win for picking an obstacle. Do not allow the patient trolley to be dragged into the fighting area.

*Owler lowers his shoulder and steps toward the doorway.*

**Enemy label:** `Owler — Night Porter`. Only one opponent.

**Turn interface intent (non-spoken):** *Owler sets himself to shove through the doorway.*

Give the player a visible first threat they can respond to using common **guard, reposition or unarmed counter** actions. An attacker winding up for a shove or strike can be sidestepped or guarded against, but the resolution follows real combat rules. Do not make guarding a magical stop to an adult man's full weight; if it fails, the player may be knocked back, hurt or lose.

After an aggressive push, Owler may be momentarily off balance or held back by the cart. This provides ordinary player counterplay. Don't script a fixed two-hit knockout or grant the Stitcher an origin-only combat skill.

## S06-C — Victory: Owler backs off

Victory is **nonlethal**: the player uses normal combat choices to make Owler unable or unwilling to force the door, without needing to kill him.

*Owler backs away from the doorway, breathing hard. He looks at the attendant standing with the trolley and at the people gathering in the ward.*

**OWLER:** “Fine. I'll tell them you stopped the transfer.”

**PLAYER:** “Tell them he refused.”

He can complain to the school; he does not suddenly become compassionate or disclose a secret surgeon's name. His paid transfer fails. The player gets a clear **combat victory**.

## S06-D — Defeat: ward staff keep the transfer stopped

Defeat is a genuine loss. Owler can overpower the Stitcher with normal combat outcomes; he must not automatically lose because the story requires the patient safe.

*Owler drives the player against the door frame. The Stitcher falls. He turns toward the ward, but the attendant has already drawn the patient trolley back behind the inner passage door. Other staff have heard the shouting.*

**ATTENDANT:** “He said no. We all heard him. You're not taking him.”

*Claudia steps into the doorway. She sees the fallen worker and the other staff gathering behind the attendant.*

**CLAUDIA:** “That's enough. You're not moving him past these witnesses.”

**OWLER:** “You'll have to answer to the school.”

**CLAUDIA:** “I'll speak to them myself. Now get out of my infirmary.”

Owler backs off **because the contested transfer is physically withheld, witnessed, and now carries professional consequences**, not because the defeated player somehow beat him. Claudia is defending her authority, not turning into an empathetic rescuer. She intends to handle the school's response herself; her established charm does not override witnesses or written evidence.

The player remains **defeated**, receives whatever actual injury/Health consequences the shared combat system determines, and is helped to the ordinary clinical service by an unnamed worker. Owler takes **no coins, clothes, bag, papers or equipment**. There is no rematch requirement and no abrupt teleporting to the rented home.

## S06-E — Close this one patient's case, both outcomes

After Owler withdraws, Claudia must record the cancellation of **this particular disputed transfer** rather than a permanent stop to all future teaching contracts. The patient is placed back in ordinary care with the requested demonstration cancelled. This step works in either outcome because of his witnessed refusal and the public struggle—not because the player obtained all three branch proofs.

**PLAYER (if conscious and present; optional, not used after an incapacitating loss):** “What happens to him?”

**CLAUDIA:** “He stays here. I'm cancelling this transfer. The school can take it up with me.”

The patient remains alive, and the unnecessary teaching demonstration does **not** occur in this episode. He is not magically healed, released from all debt or assigned a new identity. Claudia still faces missing school money and the fever ward's coal problem; neither issue is solved by defeating Owler.

The branch finding remains distinct:
- **Industry:** the receiving point demanded a contract number and no handover was completed.
- **Commerce:** the recorded thumbprint was disputed based on the sedative and witness entries.
- **Scholarship:** the notes described teaching, not needed treatment.

The patient is safe in the infirmary at the end, not left tied up or sent off-screen toward another procedure.

## Stage VI implementation — 20 November 2026

The player begins this new combat stage only after the Stage V patient refusal
has been witnessed, the attendant/Matron hold is recorded, and one doorway
preparation (`reason`, `cart` or `brace`) is saved. Those facts are verified
under the same account lock used to create the first combat turn. Stage V itself
cannot determine the outcome or award a combat reward.

**Owler uses the exact same baseline strike statistics as Flint:** 50 Health,
14–17 standard damage, 49% base accuracy, contact-range forceful blows,
the shared heavy-technique cost, fatigue/Guard/Momentum/Opening logic and
initiative system. He has **no invented truncheon or strap**; the prototype
treats his shoves and blows as blunt unarmed strikes. His only AI difference
is a higher preference for choosing the existing `guard` action on later turns
(34% baseline guard check, with an additional 35% opportunity after repeated
player attacks). Guard still uses the normal mitigation roll. No guaranteed
success, invulnerability or hidden defence modifier is introduced.

The three saved preparations use existing encounter geometry:

- **Reason:** contact range, no free combat bonus.
- **Cart:** far range, with the empty cart in the doorway; Owler must use
  the standard Move action to get close. The patient trolley is separate.
- **Brace:** contact range and the same +2 normal player readiness used by
  the Flint encounter; no automatic Guard.

Owler is a legitimate single-opponent PvE fight. A victory comes from normal
damage or withdrawal; defeat is a real loss. A completed outcome saves both
the actual `player_victory` / `player_defeat` and the patient's protection:
`patient_safe=true` and `transfer_cancelled=true`. On defeat, the staff
and Claudia enforce the already witnessed stop, without crediting the fallen
player with a win. Both endings cancel **this patient's** transfer only and
do not fix hospital funding or punish the school.

The existing shared Battle Chronicle, actions, resource HUD, 21 human
descriptive hit locations and replay persistence are reused. Each round has
one readable story plus one compact numerical summary, with no nested event
lists. The Stage V service-corridor art, standing Owler image and defeated
Owler image are reused. The route does not give loot, pay, XP or a new skill.

**Stage VII handoff:** After the fight the actual player Health, wounds and
defeat/victory remain saved. The subsequent common 10-coin clinic service
is not introduced as a broken link in Stage VI; implementing Stitcher's
Stage VII clinic is the next separate stage.
