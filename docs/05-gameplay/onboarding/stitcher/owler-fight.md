## S06-A — What the fight is actually about

Owler is a worn-out hospital porter **paid by the anatomy school to complete this one transfer**. He has a transfer paper but no uncontested handover: the patient has **refused aloud before a ward witness**, and the trolley is still inside the infirmary behind an attendant. Claudia has ordered a temporary stop, partly to protect herself and the infirmary from an obvious dispute.

Owler tries to shove his way through to retrieve the trolley or enforce the old order. The player's goal is to **keep him from forcing the transfer**. This is an ordinary, short, real **turn-based PvE** fight against a tired but capable man, not a duel with a surgeon, police force or supernatural creature.

**The player starts unarmed** with Basic Clothes, a Crude Small Bag, 20 coins and Medicine +3 with one different Major Skill +1. No free clinical weapon, injectable drug, new weapon proficiency, bonus XP or loot. Owler's possible short truncheon/leather strap is an existing **TBD choice**, not yet a fixed combat item.

## S06-C — Victory: Owler backs off

Victory is **nonlethal**: the player uses normal combat choices to make Owler unable or unwilling to force the door, without needing to kill him.

*Owler backs away from the doorway, breathing hard. He looks at the attendant standing with the trolley and at the people gathering in the ward.*

**OWLER:** “Fine. I'll tell them you wouldn't let me through.”

**PLAYER:** “Tell them whatever you want.”

He can complain to the school; he does not suddenly become compassionate or disclose a secret surgeon's name. His paid transfer fails. The player gets a clear **combat victory**.

## S06-D — Defeat: ward staff keep the transfer stopped

Defeat is a genuine loss. Owler can overpower the Stitcher with normal combat outcomes; he must not automatically lose because the story requires the patient safe.

*Owler drives the player against the door frame. The Stitcher falls. He turns toward the ward, but the attendant has already drawn the patient trolley back behind the inner passage door. Other staff have heard the shouting.*

**ATTENDANT:** “He's not leaving. We all heard him say no.”

*Claudia steps into the doorway and sees the fallen worker and the gathered witnesses.*

**CLAUDIA:** “This brawl can raise some questions. Get out now!”

**OWLER:** “You'll answer for the school, then.”

**CLAUDIA:** “Know your place! I'll just smile to them. Get out!”

Owler backs off **because the contested transfer is physically withheld, witnessed, and now carries professional consequences**, not because the defeated player somehow beat him. Claudia is defending her authority, not turning into an empathetic rescuer. She expects to smooth over the school's questions with her appearance and charm, which she knowingly uses to escape scrutiny; this is her belief, not a guarantee that a smile defeats witnessed objections or written evidence.

The player remains **defeated**, receives whatever actual injury/Health consequences the shared combat system determines, and is helped to the ordinary clinical service by an unnamed worker. Owler takes **no coins, clothes, bag, papers or equipment**. There is no rematch requirement and no abrupt teleporting to the rented home.
