## A. The Night Trolley — Industry / Hauling

The player stays near the patient while ward equipment and a secured transfer trolley are taken along a hospital service passage. The trolley and keys remain hospital property; it is not a player vehicle.

**Ordinary Hauling interactions:** [Check the load against the docket] → [Arrange equipment to keep the trolley stable] → choose the short, narrow passage or a longer, roomier one → [Compare the receiving mark to the dispatch note]. Both routes work, but require different handling time. The player does not sign away the patient or complete a final handover.

*At the receiving door, a clerk looks at the papers rather than the patient.*

**RECEIVER:** “Which contract?”

**PLAYER:** “There's a man here. Doesn't he have a name?”

**RECEIVER:** “I asked for the number.”

**PLAYER:** “I'm not handing him over yet.”

**RECEIVER:** “Then go back to the ward.”

**Branch finding:** The receiving theatre requests the patient by contract reference rather than name. The player knows the service door and the required official mark; the final transfer is still pending. The receiving worker need not know whether the patient consented.

**[Record handover pending]** finishes the movement job without granting pay. The patient can be moved back toward the ward/service corridor while the dispute is unresolved.

## B. The Signed Consent — Commerce / Contracts

The player challenges the authority to move the patient. An unnamed ward records clerk opens the ordinary charity-bond and consent paperwork.

**Ordinary Contracts interactions:** [Match the consent paper to the patient record] → [Check the medicine and witnessing entries] → choose to attach the timing record or bring a witness who heard the patient object → [Mark the transfer disputed].

The entries show a sleeping draught was administered **before the thumbprint was registered**. The paper cannot be accepted as proof of willing agreement merely because it bears a print. Do not require a forged signature or secret high-level Contracts skill.

**CLERK:** “There's his mark on the paper.”

**PLAYER:** “He says they took it while he slept.”

**CLERK:** “Let's see the medicine book.”

*After checking it:*

**PLAYER:** “They'd given him the draught before they took his print.”

**CLERK:** “I'll put it down as disputed.”

*Claudia sees the amended entry.*

**CLAUDIA:** “You might have come to me first.”

**PLAYER:** “Would you have listened?”

**CLAUDIA:** “Well, the school won't pay while that's on the page.”

**Branch finding:** The ward record shows the mark was entered while he was sedated. A genuine witnessed objection provides a procedural hold that Claudia can act on. She resents the financial loss; her looks and charm cannot change a written contradiction. This does not amount to a court ruling or a citywide ban.

**[File the disputed entry]** closes the job. The written hold exists, but Owler may still be acting on the original transfer instructions.

## C. Notes for the Living — Scholarship / Investigation

The player asks an unnamed records copyist to compare the patient's treatment notes with the scheduled teaching demonstration. No new doctor or special investigation office is introduced.

**Ordinary Investigation interactions:** [Compare the admission note with care already given] → [Match the teaching schedule's reference] → choose which pages to copy and cross-reference → [Record the difference between treatment and instruction].

**COPYIST:** “Which pages?”

**PLAYER:** “His treatment. And tomorrow's lesson.”

*After the comparison:*

**PLAYER:** “There's nothing here saying he needs another operation.”

**COPYIST:** “No. He's down for instruction.”

**PLAYER:** “Not for his own wound, then?”

**COPYIST:** “Not according to these notes.”

**Branch finding:** The clinical casebook does not establish a further needed procedure; the new demonstration is scheduled for instruction alone. Accurate copies give the player grounds to challenge the handover. They do not prove when the thumbprint was taken—that is exclusive to the Contracts route.

**[Finish the comparison note]** closes the job. The copying is work-order evidence, not a saleable rare manuscript.
