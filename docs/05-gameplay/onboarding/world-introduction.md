# THE PALL

Coal smoke hangs over Grand Cylinder City long before the morning shifts begin.

Behind the enormous stone walls, hundreds of workshops, foundries and factories stand among crowded homes, narrow streets and old municipal buildings. Above it all rises the Grand Cylinder, visible from nearly every quarter of the city. A vast structure of stone and iron, it provides the city with power and heat, at the cost of thousands of workers' sweat and toil.

Almost an entire country's surviving population now lives within these walls.

Nearly a century ago, enormous ruptures across the world released toxic fumes and strange dust in a catastrophe remembered as **The Plume**. Nobody knows what caused it, but its effects on the land and living things remain, while the oldest survivors still recall the hunger, displacement and desperate years that followed.

Some live well, with warm homes and full tables. Most have considerably less.

Yet the city survives.

Its people build, repair, trade, steal, craft, help one another, venture beyond the walls and do whatever else they must to survive.

The protected outskirts contain the remaining farms, settlements and patches of usable land. Farther away begin the Grey Marches, where dangers lurk and reliable roads become scarce.

And farther still... **the Pall.**

Much of that land remains uncharted. Strange materials, abandoned places and things not yet understood have been found by those who ventured beyond the protected lands. Some expeditions returned with valuable discoveries. Others came back with injured or dead companions.

Some never returned at all.

There are still places no one has properly surveyed.

Some people will earn enough.

Others will have to find another way, however much they fear what lies beyond the walls.

**This is Grand Cylinder City.**

**And this is where your life continues.**
