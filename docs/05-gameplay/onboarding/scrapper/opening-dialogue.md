# Scrapper — Stage 1: Ignatius Smog at Rust Yard (APPROVED)

**Status: APPROVED AND LOCKED (2026-10-16) — user approved the plain-language revision.** The user found the earlier Scrapper work too technical to understand. This version puts **plain physical actions in speech and UI**. The [locked Scrapper storyline](README.md), character name, persistent Rust Yard setting, first job **Strip a Condemned Pump**, Smog's unmistakable cough and his exact phrase **“that elitist bourgeois luxury”** have NOT changed.

## What the player sees

*Rust Yard is full of broken iron and old machines. At the edge of the yard sits a large water pump beside an open drain. Its outer stand has a couple of old metal plates attached.*

*A rough cough comes from behind a pile of scrap. Ignatius Smog walks over with a work slip.*

**SMOG:** “That old pump by the drain. Take the metal plates off its stand. Leave the big pipe alone.”

**PLAYER (optional):** “You ought to get away from this smoke awhile.”

**SMOG (optional reply):** “Fresh air? I've no time for that elitist bourgeois luxury.”

**SMOG (convergence, also if the optional exchange is skipped):** “Keep the good pieces. Broken ones go in the scrap heap. I'll check when you're done.”

**[Take the work slip]**

The Smog dialogue is short on purpose. He and the player already work here; he doesn't lecture the Scrapper about how pumps work. His exact unusual line about fresh air remains the one intentional fancy phrase in his otherwise plain manner.

## What is the job?

The player has been told to **remove only old metal pieces from the outside of the pump's stand**, check which ones can still be used and put the rest with scrap. Those plates are explicitly **nonessential**: the player is not being asked to remove protective covers from moving machinery or anything holding back water.

The player must **not** remove the big drainage pipe, open the pump's working body or stop anything that could still be running. This limited instruction matters for Stage 3, when the apparently abandoned pump turns out to be serving people who live below.

**Work slip (ordinary interface, simple words):**

> **Job: Strip a Condemned Pump**  
> Take the old metal plates off the pump's stand.  
> Keep pieces that are still good. Put broken pieces with the scrap.  
> **Don't touch the main pipe.**  
> This yard clean-up job is **unpaid**.

“Condemned” is part of the already-approved job name: the yard has **marked the pump for scrap**. The screen shows the plain instructions immediately; it does not assume the player knows what “condemned” means.

**[Begin work]** — enters the [ordinary Mechanics interaction in Stage 2](condemned-pump-interaction.md).

## Economy and continuity

- This first assignment is **yard clean-up of its own scrap**, not a customer-funded paid job. The work slip shows **0 coins / 0 ordinary XP** before the player begins. There is no surprise withheld wage after finishing.
- Regular, repeatable Mechanics jobs at Rust Yard are **paid normally** and give ordinary XP. The zero-payout rule belongs only to the scripted tutorial task.
- The yard provides its own spanner and any other small work supplies, and keeps the salvaged metal afterward. Nothing is added to the player's inventory.
- The Scrapper begins with the common **Basic Clothes, Crude Small Bag and 20 coins**, and **Mechanics +3** granted at character creation.
- **The Last Dry Floor** reveal, the warning cloth, the three knocks, the real running load, the hidden residents, the second skill and Barnaby Rusk all wait for their later approved-in-outline stages.
- Smog's cough is a characterization detail, **not** proof of a particular medical condition, quest or Pall exposure.

All were accurate-ish workshop shorthand but asked a non-mechanical player to translate the task before they could picture it. The official job title is kept because it is locked; the subtitle and work directions explain what to do.
