# Scrapper — Stage 1: Ignatius Smog at Rust Yard (PROPOSED)

**Status: PROPOSED FOR USER APPROVAL (2026-10-16); NOT LOCKED.** Rust Yard, **Ignatius Smog, Yard Foreman**, his unmistakable cough and the exact phrase **"that elitist bourgeois luxury"** about fresh air are established in the [locked Scrapper overview](README.md). This draft proposes his first spoken exchange and the handoff to the already-locked repeatable **Mechanics job, Strip a Condemned Pump**. The unusual drainage problem, the cloth warning and the three knocks belong **only to Stage 3 — The Last Dry Floor**.

## S01 — The condemned lot

*At Rust Yard, a hard cough comes from behind a row of discarded pump housings. Ignatius Smog steps into view, holding a salvage docket. He coughs again before speaking.*

**SMOG:** “That pump by the culvert wall. Take the outside fittings off. Leave the main pipe alone.”

**PLAYER (optional):** “You ought to get away from this smoke awhile.”

**SMOG (optional reply):** “Fresh air? I've no time for that elitist bourgeois luxury.”

**SMOG (convergence, whether or not the optional exchange is played):** “Sort the good fittings from the scrap. I'll look at the tally after.”

**[Take the salvage docket]** — open the normal Mechanics job card for the condemned auxiliary pump's **exterior-only** salvage lot, with the approved first-work actions in [Stage 2](condemned-pump-interaction.md).

This is a familiar foreman speaking to an already-employed yard worker. He has no reason to explain what a pump is or where they both work. His cough and the smoky yard are enough to make the optional exchange sensible, but it is **not a newly diagnosed condition, quest, debuff or sign of Pall contamination**. The politically pompous turn of phrase is his **already approved personal joke**, delivered once without an invented second catchphrase.

## How wages are communicated without a false promise

The **job docket**, not a long foreman speech, makes the exceptional pay state clear **before the work starts**:

- **Work order:** Yard's own condemned-stock clearance.
- **Pay for this particular clearance:** **0 coins; 0 ordinary Mechanics XP** (tutorial exception). The yard is clearing its own stock, not fulfilling a customer-paid piecework order. There is no pending wage cheque to recover from the foreman later.
- **Ordinary repeatable versions:** salvage commissions at Rust Yard remain normal **paid Mechanics jobs with ordinary XP**. This no-pay handling is specific to the scripted first session.
- **Tools:** yard-owned tools available for use during the work. No tool, special clothing, weapon or salvaged component is granted permanently.

The player's starting state remains **Mechanics +3** at character creation, STR/DEX/INT/RES all 5, Health 50, Basic Clothes, one Crude Small Bag and 20 coins. No skill reward or item drops from taking the docket.

## Character and narrative guardrails

- **Ignatius is coarse, worn by smoke and direct.** His speech is simple and practical, except for his one intentionally lofty joke about fresh air. Do not imitate Claudia's cruelty, Guther's evasiveness or Flint's limited vocabulary.
- **He does not know everything:** his official salvage docket says the pump is condemned. He is not presently established as the architect of an evacuation plan, a landlord, a municipal engineer or a conspirator.
- **No early revelation:** neither player nor Ignatius announces that somebody is living beneath Rust Yard, mentions the warning cloth or hears the deliberate knocks. No pressure dial discovery is shown in this scene.
- **Plausible safety instruction:** "Leave the main pipe alone" establishes the limited salvage scope from the start, rather than requiring the character to strip a live drainage link without authorization.
- **Interaction pacing:** one short optional exchange, a single common final instruction and entry into repeatable Mechanics work. No dialogue-tree interrogation about tools, numerics or mechanics.
- **The foreman's unusual rhetoric is locked, but the precise new wording and staging here are NOT approved yet.** No invented family, past illness or future reunion is established.
