**Status: APPROVED AND LOCKED AT ENCOUNTER LEVEL (2026-10-16).** The user approved the genuine nonlethal fight, route-independent pump safety, brief win/lose dialogue and worker intervention after defeat. Barnaby's identity, paid copper motive and the **pump remains uncut** ending were previously locked in the [Scrapper master](README.md); [Stage 5](barnaby-confrontation.md) is also approved. **Turn-based combat rules and numerical values remain TBD.**

## S06-A — Where the fight starts

*Barnaby steps at the Scrapper with his work tool in hand. Behind him, the red handle marked STOP PUMP is still untouched. Ignatius Smog and two other yard workers are nearby.*

Barnaby wants to **get past the Scrapper, stop the old pump and cut the copper pipe he has been promised payment for**. The player wants the pump to continue running so the rooms below do not flood. Smog has already told Barnaby **not to cut the pipe** and the other yard workers have put their tools aside. This order is a credible reason the pump can remain protected in either combat outcome, even though Barnaby won't accept losing his own pay.

- **Enemy display:** **Barnaby Rusk — Scrap Worker** (working combat role, final UI naming TBD).
- **One human opponent** with a short, modest iron hand tool of the kind used at Rust Yard. **Exact tool/weapon statistics and even the precise implement remain TBD**; do not create a collectable reward weapon.
- The Scrapper starts with **Basic Clothes, Crude Small Bag, 20 coins**, **Mechanics +3** from character creation and exactly **+1 Industry, Fieldcraft or Commerce** from the already-selected Stage 3 branch. No starter weapon, combat-skill bonus or free tools.
- Barnaby is not a city guard, a company boss, a supernatural threat, or the game's main villain. He is a yard worker angry about losing an expected day's earnings.
- The fight is **real short turn-based PvE** with a reachable win and a reachable defeat; it is not an automatic scripted victory or a reflex minigame.

## S06-C — Ordinary combat tutorial beats

**Visible intention (UI, not spoken):** *Barnaby draws back his arm for a hard swing.*

This clear threat gives a beginner a reason to try ordinary **guard, reposition or unarmed attack** actions. Defending or stepping clear may help under the common combat system, but no magical auto-parry or predetermined miss is allowed.

A poor decision can lead to genuine Health loss or defeat. A good decision can create a chance to counterattack when Barnaby is off balance or moving around the bin. The enemy must react to the player's real actions rather than simply conceding after a scripted tutorial countdown.

- Keep the fight area **between Barnaby and the pump controls**, not beside the open water or inside the residents' rooms.
- The player is not required to operate the red handle or repair the pump mid-battle.
- The other yard workers stay outside the fighting space; they do not secretly attack Barnaby for the player to create a false win.
- Barnaby cannot disable the pump with an uninterruptible hidden action during the fight. The local story preserves the already-approved **uncut pump** outcome; his aim is to force the player aside, but the shared combat win/lose result resolves this physical clash before any separate machine-operation step.
- Neither player nor Barnaby needs to be killed. A victory drives Barnaby off or leaves him unable to press the attack; the exact common combat end rule is TBD.

## S06-D — Player victory

*Barnaby backs away from the walkway. He holds his arm close and looks at the other workers, none of whom are coming forward to help him cut the pipe.*

**BARNABY:** “I was promised that copper.”

**PLAYER:** “You'll have to leave it.”

**BARNABY:** “Aye. So it seems.”

*Barnaby goes back through the yard without taking the copper. The red handle stays where it was. The pump continues moving water.*

This is **a real win in the fight**: the player stopped a man who was trying to force his way to the pump. It does not earn copper, cancel the city's scrap order or fix the residents' living conditions.

## S06-E — Player defeat

A **real loss** happens if Barnaby overcomes the player through normal combat. The player is knocked down or left unable to defend themself. The game must record defeat, not silently switch to a win just because the tutorial needs to continue.

*Barnaby shoves the Scrapper down beside the iron bin. He turns towards the red handle. Smog and the same two workers, who have been watching, step between Barnaby and the pump.*

**SMOG:** “Enough, Barnaby. I told you to leave that pipe.”

**BARNABY:** “He came at me!”

**SMOG:** “And now he's down. Put that tool away.”

**BARNABY:** “Who's paying me for this?”

**SMOG:** “Take it up with whoever promised you the money. You're done here.”

*One of the workers helps the Scrapper up. The others stay by the controls until Barnaby leaves.*

This works because **Ignatius already ordered the work stopped in Stage 5, the ordinary cutting crew were already standing aside, and the workers were visibly nearby before the first blow**. They intervene when Barnaby beats another yard worker and moves to disobey the foreman's order. They do not appear magically after a loss and do not retroactively win the fight on the player's behalf. Barnaby knows continued fighting against a group of his fellow workers would get him no closer to his pay.

The pump is still running and the copper pipe has **not** been cut. Barnaby takes **no coins, bag, clothes, notes or items** from the player. The player has definitely **lost** this fight and may have genuine medical injuries, but there is no fail-state forcing a restart, no unavoidable death of the residents, and no bonus rescue reward.
