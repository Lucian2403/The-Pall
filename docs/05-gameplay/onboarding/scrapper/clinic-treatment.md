**Status: PROPOSED FOR USER APPROVAL (2026-10-16); NOT LOCKED.** The ordinary **10-coin** clinic service and its place between the introductory fight and the single universal rent scene are already [locked for every background](../shared-structure.md). ## Getting to the clinic

After the fight at Rust Yard, the pump **still runs** and no one has cut its copper pipe. The residents below have a little more time, but their housing problem is not solved.

- **If the player won:** Barnaby leaves. The Scrapper can walk to an ordinary city clinician. Any actual Health loss or injury comes from the real combat result.
- **If the player lost:** Smog and the yard workers kept Barnaby from turning off the pump; an unnamed worker helps the defeated Scrapper to ordinary care. The game records **defeat**, not victory, and does not confiscate starting coins or belongings.
- **Both paths:** the player has **20 coins** and the common **Basic Clothes / Crude Small Bag** before the clinic charge. No loot, wage, XP or weapon is awarded by the first two jobs or fight.
- The ordinary medical provider is labelled **CLINICIAN**. Give them no new personal name or invented clinic building. This is the **same Care / Treatment service** available to the other backgrounds, not a Scrapper-only medical system.

## S07-A — Short, plain dialogue

*The clinician looks up as the Scrapper comes in.*

**CLINICIAN:** “What happened?”

**PLAYER:** “Had a fight with another worker at the yard.”

**CLINICIAN:** “Sit down. Let me have a look.”

**[Examine condition]** — display the player's **real** current Health, injuries (if any) and ordinary care options. Do not force a cut or a bruise just because the scene follows a fight.

*After the examination, the clinician shows what can be treated and the price.*

**PLAYER:** “How much?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins. That covers the examination and any basic care you need.”

**PLAYER:** “All right. Go ahead.”

**[Pay 10 coins and accept care]**

The scene is ordinary and unshowy. The clinician doesn't ask about the under-yard residents or resolve the city's drainage problem in conversation. The Scrapper doesn't need to describe copper-cutting machinery or read a medical report to know what to do.

## S07-B — Reusable medical work

1. **[Examine condition]** — show the actual Health state and any visible combat conditions, with understandable labels. A clean victory can leave the player unhurt; a defeat can require assistance and care. No wound is added behind the scenes.
2. **[Review care and price]** — show the basic service and **10 coins** before payment. Only treatment that suits the player's real condition can be offered. Do not claim every blow causes a cut or that every injury needs a bandage. The effective amount of Health restored and medical time cost remain TBD.
3. **[Speak to clinician]** — the exchange above; no alternate dialogue based solely on the word “victory” if the character's injury state doesn't support it.
4. **[Pay 10 coins and accept care]** — deduct **exactly 10 coins once**, from 20 to **10 coins**, for the established tutorial service. Save clinic completion and the actual medical result together so a reload cannot charge a second time.
5. **[Leave the clinic]** — resume ordinary movement toward the **same shared rented home** as all five other origins. Only there does the rent notice appear.

The ordinary recurring medical service remains reusable throughout the city; non-tutorial prices, recovery rules, examination-only cases and skill interactions are defined globally, not by the Scrapper scene. This scripted visit grants **0 XP and no loot**.

### A character who took no damage

The shared first-session design calls for a **10-coin clinical visit**, but a well-played fight may involve no injuries. Do **not** quietly add a scrape to make the price seem justified. How a perfectly healthy character is offered a meaningful paid examination is an **open issue shared by all six backgrounds**. This Stage 7 draft doesn't decide that question or change the previously locked tutorial economy.

## S07-C — One shared Stage 8, not a separate ending

After the clinic, the player goes home with **10 coins** and plays the one [already approved shared Stage 8](../shared-home-rent-ending.md). It is **identical** across all backgrounds:

- **[Enter home]** → **[Read the rent notice]**. The notice shows **150 coins owing**, an amount still marked **PROVISIONAL** until the housing system is finalized.
- **[Put the notice down]** → the player says exactly: **“Ah, hell. Need a place of my own. First I've got this rent to pay. More jobs... or I could make something to sell.”**
- The player remains in the ordinary common rented home, with free access to the recurring jobs, crafting and city activities. No mandatory new Scrapper job or secret quest begins.

**Do not author a copied Scrapper Stage 8 file**, a different notice, a different line, a landlord NPC, or a different rented room. Stage 8 belongs to one shared canonical document.
