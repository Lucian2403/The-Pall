## I03-A — An extra page

**Trigger:** only after the player has completed the usual [Winter Register](winter-register-interaction.md) work: compared both lists, dealt with the blurred entry, handed Erwin the clean copy, returned the old books as instructed and had the ordinary task closed. This supplementary sheet never appears in the normal copy/verification choices and is not a repeatable discovery on future paid office work.

*Erwin opens a separate winter volume to put the finished copy with the office papers. A thin folded sheet slips from between its pages and lands beside the player's hand. It is not part of the clean household copy just finished.*

**[Pick up the loose sheet]**

*The sheet has two lists side by side: houses in the poorer ward, and how much coal is to be sent to each. Beside them someone has written how many people may die if the fuel is reduced.*

**[Read the heading]** — its earlier heading was:

> **EXPECTED WINTER LOSSES**

*The word* **EXPECTED** *has been crossed out in a firmer hand. In its place is written:*

> **AUTHORIZED**

*One clear line below reads, exactly:*

> **WARD SIX — REDUCED HEAT / ACCEPTABLE LOSSES: NINE.**

The user understands the distinction without a law degree: the original column estimated how many might die; somebody has changed the writing to make those deaths look like something **permitted**. **Nine** is the approved projected-loss figure in this one Ward Six entry—not a confirmed death count, a supernatural prediction, a global city rule or nine people already dead. The scene does not identify the writer of **AUTHORIZED** or prove this is legally valid.

**[Check the coal column]** — the fuel figures for those homes are being cut, and the loss number sits beside the reduction. The exact amount of coal and all personal names remain TBD; the player is not asked to infer actual individual deaths from a generic formula.

The loose paper is a **quest finding**, not a tradeable item. The official records stay in the copying office. Copies/notes needed to follow one route are work records, not personal starter loot.

## I03-B — Ask Erwin what this means

*Erwin notices the loose sheet in the player's hand. He reaches out for it, then leaves his hand resting on the desk.*

**PLAYER:** “What's this doing in the winter book?”

**ERWIN:** “That is not for the public copy. Give it here, if you please.”

**PLAYER:** “It says nine people may die when they cut the coal.”

**ERWIN:** “An estimate. They're estimates, these figures.”

**PLAYER:** “Then why has somebody written ‘authorized’ over ‘expected’?”

*Erwin adjusts the cuff of his coat.*

**ERWIN:** “I imagine the gentlemen responsible have considered the matter.”

**PLAYER:** “Do you know who wrote it?”

**ERWIN:** “I wasn't invited to their table.”

**PLAYER:** “But you'll copy it for them?”

**ERWIN:** “We copy what we're sent. We do not sit in judgment upon the council.”

**PLAYER:** “They're talking about people freezing.”

**ERWIN:** “And what would you have me do? March in there and lose us the work? We'd all be out on our arses by week's end.”

*He looks at the open book, then straightens the loose sheet.*

**ERWIN:** “The clean copy is done. Leave this sheet with me. You've other work to see to.”

Erwin believes polished manners and official rank deserve deference, even as the meaning of the page clearly unsettles his orderly world. His language drops abruptly at the prospect of losing money and status. He is protecting the municipal contract that pays for ordinary office work; his fear is real, but it does not make the document harmless or establish that he approved it. Nor does he reveal a secret author or suddenly offer to lead a resistance.

**Optional [Look at the two words again]** brings the crossed-out heading back on-screen. It cannot award an extra skill or expose information reserved for another branch.

## I03-C — Choose what to check next

The player is not offered an abstract “good versus evil” slider. These are three different practical ways to respond to the same paper:

| Button | What the player is trying to learn or do | Different Major Skill / Stage 4 job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Challenge the order]** | Find out whether a winter-heating appeal and the signed papers give the city any right to leave people without coal | **Commerce +1** / Contracts — **The Coal Waiver** |
| **[Check the houses]** | Go to the listed addresses and learn whether people actually live there and how a coal cart could reach them | **Fieldcraft +1** / Navigation — **The Door Count** |
| **[Follow the coal delivery]** | Check the cart that's supposed to bring coal to Ward Six and see whether its load matches the paper | **Industry +1** / Hauling — **The Short Cart** |

**Choice-specific player responses (approved exact dialogue):**

A. **PLAYER:** “I'll go to the coal office. I want to see who signed this.”

B. **PLAYER:** “I'm going to those houses. Somebody ought to count the people who live there.”

C. **PLAYER:** “I'll find the coal cart. Those people still need coal this winter.”

Erwin's response is identical to all three:

**ERWIN:** “Very well. Only don't go telling them I sent you. I most certainly did not.”

He wants the copying office left out of a potentially embarrassing dispute. He has not commissioned an extra paid job or approved the action; the player goes to an existing workplace service/route to make an urgent check. No invented confession or wages.
