# Inkbound — Stage 7: Clinic After the Fuel Watchman (PROPOSED)

**Status: PROPOSED FOR USER APPROVAL (2026-10-19); NOT LOCKED.** Inkbound [Stage 5](sealed-stove-confrontation.md) and [Stage 6](silas-pumpard-fight.md) are APPROVED AND LOCKED at scene/encounter level, while the ordinary **10-coin** medical visit, original 20-coin starting amount and the [identical shared Stage 8](../shared-home-rent-ending.md) are already locked for every origin. This page proposes **the precise Inkbound clinic dialogue** and applies the existing reusable Care / Treatment flow, without adding a special clinic, medical effect or new named character.

## S07-A — Leave Ward Six and seek care

Silas Pumpard has stopped attacking, and the ordinary residents have already heard the one finding from the player's Stage 4 route. The communal coal box remains locked, no matter who won the fight. The winter-heating order is still in force. This is a local incident, not a campaign-wide victory.

- **Player victory:** Silas withdrew from the confrontation. The Inkbound may travel to a normal city clinician. Winning does **not** guarantee the player is uninjured; display the actual Health state from the real fight.
- **Player defeat:** Silas drove the Inkbound away while retaining his place beside the locked coal box. The unnamed resident/neighbour who helped the player after the loss may assist them to ordinary care. The defeat stays recorded and must never be reclassified as a victory just to progress the tutorial.
- **Both paths:** the player still has **20 coins, Basic Clothes and a Crude Small Bag** before this consultation, together with Scholarship +3 and the one previously granted +1 in Commerce, Fieldcraft or Industry. Silas took no money or belongings; the initial jobs and fight offered zero wages, XP or loot.
- The provider is an **unnamed CLINICIAN** at a regular city care service. No exclusive Inkbound treatment location, Erwin appearance, new clinician name or special combat-injury item is established.

## S07-B — Dialogue (proposed exact lines)

*At the ordinary clinic, a clinician looks up as the Inkbound comes inside.*

**CLINICIAN:** “What happened?”

**PLAYER:** “Had a fight with a watchman in Ward Six. He tried to take my papers.”

**CLINICIAN:** “Sit here. I'll have a look.”

**[Examine condition]** — show the character's **real** Health and conditions, whether or not any wounds exist. The clinician does not declare a cut, bruise, broken bone or other injury merely because there was a fight.

*After the examination, the ordinary service screen shows the available care and the price.*

**PLAYER:** “How much is the visit?”

**CLINICIAN:** “Ten coins. That includes the examination and any basic care you need.”

**PLAYER:** “All right. Go ahead.”

**[Pay 10 coins and accept care]**

The clinician is interested in examining the patient, not in arguing over coal policy or condemning the watchman. The player does not show the Ward Six papers, request an official verdict, or tell the clinician about the “authorized” deaths page. Keep spoken lines plain and literal, in the game-wide 19th-century-inspired style.

## S07-C — Ordinary city care interface, no invented special rules

1. **[Examine condition]** shows current and maximum Health and all actual wounds/conditions caused by the finished fight, with understandable labels. If the player took no damage, the displayed condition is accurately **unhurt**. No compulsory injury is invented.
2. **[Review care and price]** shows the ordinary basic examination/treatment option and **10 coins** before the player agrees. Present the real expected effect, not an unconditional promise of full Health or a guaranteed bandage.
3. **[Pay 10 coins and accept care]** deducts **10 coins once**, changing the balance from **20 to 10** for the scripted tutorial. Apply only appropriate Health/condition effects from the shared system, which remains numerically TBD. Save payment and completion together; reloads cannot charge again.
4. **[Leave the clinic]** resumes ordinary city travel. No additional Inkbound-only job, fuel handout, Silas rematch or new named NPC interrupts the trip home.

This is the **same provider service used by Millhand, Bellrunner, Squatter, Stitcher and Scrapper**, not a separate Investigation or Scholarship job. Being a copyist does not grant Medicine experience or a special price. No XP or item reward is awarded.

### Perfectly unhurt player (shared open issue)

A genuinely flawless fight may leave the Inkbound with no wounds. The tutorial's common economy presently sets the examination/clinic visit to ten coins for every origin, but **the shared Health design has not fully decided how an examination-only visit should be handled when treatment is unnecessary**. Do not silently create damage or invent a new medical rule in this background. The precise treatment benefits, time, and final handling of that edge case remain common-system TBD.

## S07-D — Hand off to the single shared Stage 8

When care is complete, the player returns to the exact **same rented starter home** that all six backgrounds use. Stage 8 is one [already approved shared document](../shared-home-rent-ending.md), not a new Inkbound scene:

**[Enter home] → [Read the rent notice]**

> **RENT**  
> **150 coins owing.**

The **150 coins** are PROVISIONAL pending the later game-wide rent design, not a new firm deadline or penalty. The player's normal balance after the established visit is **10 coins**.

**[Put the notice down]**

**PLAYER (same exact locked speech for every origin):** “Ah, hell. Need a place of my own. First I've got this rent to pay. More jobs... or I could make something to sell.”

After the line, the normal housing/earnings/crafting game opens. Do **not** create an Inkbound-only rent notice, alternate self-talk, another payment demand, a city official demanding back pay, or an automatic main-campaign quest.
