# Inkbound — Stage 6: Silas Pumpard at the Coal Box (PROPOSED)

**Status: PROPOSED FOR USER APPROVAL (2026-10-17); NOT LOCKED.** A short, realistic turn-based PvE fight with the named **Silas Pumpard, Fuel Watchman**, nonlethal victory and survivable defeat were already locked at story-outline level in [Inkbound's master](README.md). The detailed [Stage 5 — The Sealed Stove / The Coal Hook](sealed-stove-confrontation.md) is proposed and sets the confrontation. Stage 3 and Stage 4 are approved. **All combat numbers, equipment stats, wounds and AI still belong to the shared TBD combat system.**

## I06-A — Why the fight happens

Silas is paid to guard the communal coal allotment and its padlocked box outside a cold tenement. He has orders not to release fuel without authorization. Several residents are standing nearby. The Inkbound has refused to hand over the papers showing **one** of the three findings about Ward Six.

*Silas raises an arm, trying to shove the Inkbound away from the box. His short work hook is in his other hand. Behind him, the padlock and official seal remain intact.*

The player wants **to keep the evidence and prevent intimidation from silencing the residents**. The goal is **not** to knock down a guard so the player can break the lock or loot coal. Neither victory nor defeat grants an official winter fuel allotment; that requires a later, separate decision beyond this introductory episode.

- **One low-level human enemy:** **Silas Pumpard — Fuel Watchman**. He is a contractor protecting his wages and position, not a supernatural creature, military commander or city official.
- **Player start:** unarmed, Basic Clothes, Crude Small Bag, 20 coins, Scholarship +3 and exactly one Stage 3 +1 in Commerce, Fieldcraft or Industry. No starting combat weapon or bonus attack skill.
- **Silas's hook:** work implement in scene; final combat weapon class, balance, whether he actually strikes with it, and any disarm/drop rules are shared-system TBD. He does not surrender a hook as tutorial loot.
- **Location:** small area of street outside tenement, with the coal box as a real obstacle. Residents remain visible **but do not join as free combat allies**.
- Both combat **victory and defeat are reachable and recorded honestly**. No fixed two-turn win or guarantee that a Scholar is too weak to win.

## I06-B — Carry the Stage 5 positioning choice into combat

| Stage 5 action | What the battle actually starts with |
| --- | --- |
| **[Stand your ground]** | Player is close to Silas, between him and the nearest resident, with limited space to retreat |
| **[Step behind the coal box]** | The solid box interrupts a straight approach, so Silas must move around its side to reach the player; no automatic cover bonuses invented |
| **[Tell him to leave the papers alone]** | Player has a little more distance and room to step away but is exposed in the open street |

These scene differences feed the ordinary turn-based **reposition / guard / unarmed strike** actions. Exact movement distances and defensiveness remain TBD. The box cannot be thrown or opened as a special free attack.

**Shown threat (UI, not spoken):** *Silas brings his shoulder forward and reaches for the papers.*

Give the new player a genuine first turn and an understandable chance to defend or step aside. Silas may be unsteady after an aggressive move, creating regular counterplay. If player decisions are poor, Silas can hit or knock them down. Never simulate a loss as a "win with less Health" or have Silas silently concede just to finish the tutorial.

## I06-C — Victory: Silas backs away

The Inkbound uses the ordinary combat rules to make Silas unable or unwilling to continue the attack. The outcome is nonlethal; Silas can retreat without being killed or robbed.

*Silas backs away from the Inkbound. He looks at the residents watching him. He has not got the papers, and the coal box is still locked.*

**SILAS:** “Fine. Keep your bloody papers.”

**PLAYER:** “Then leave these people alone.”

**SILAS:** “You think they'll thank you when the coal's still locked up?”

*Silas steps away down the street, nursing his arm. Nobody opens the sealed box.*

This is a **true combat victory** and a temporary end to intimidation. The player has not overturned the order or made Silas recognize the cruelty of the figures. His accusation stings because **the cold tenement and locked fuel remain**. The residents are free to speak about what they've learned for the moment; the watchman's official duty and the city's fuel policy are not permanently abolished.

## I06-D — Defeat: Silas keeps the box, but the incident stops

Defeat must be real: the Inkbound can be beaten through the actual turn-based combat. The player ends down or unable to defend themself. They do **not** receive a retroactive victory when bystanders interrupt.

*Silas knocks the Inkbound against the side of the coal box. The player falls. He stands over them, breathing hard.*

**SILAS:** “Should've gone when I told you.”

*The resident from the Sealed Stove scene comes forward, still keeping their distance.*

**RESIDENT:** “That's enough. You've got your lock. What more do you want?”

**SILAS:** “They wouldn't give me the paper.”

**RESIDENT:** “Well, you've knocked them down. We all saw.”

*Silas looks at the other people gathered in the street and at the still-unbroken seal.*

**SILAS:** “Get them out of here. I'm not having this laid at my door.”

*He steps back towards the locked box while the resident and an unnamed neighbour help the defeated Inkbound away.*

Silas stops **because he has already protected the lock and now faces witnesses to a public beating**, not because the player somehow defeated him. Importantly, **he does not need to flee or renounce his watchman post** on defeat. He stays by the padlock and the player is driven away from the immediate area. This is meaningfully worse than winning the fight but allows the tutorial to proceed without murdering the player or erasing evidence.

He takes **no coins, clothes, bag, journal findings or physical starter gear**. The player receives actual Health/injury effects from the shared combat engine; the helpers are ordinary bystanders, not newly named NPCs or a permanent party. The signed papers, appeal note, route record or coal tally are not destroyed: the job findings remain at their originating office and in the quest record rather than in a confiscatable bag.

## I06-E — One local ending, three distinct findings

The local incident closes when the physical confrontation ends and the player moves towards regular clinical care. **Neither outcome opens the communal coal box** or supplies all the homes. The same disturbing nine-loss page is still part of the original office's records; the author of **AUTHORIZED** remains unknown.

| Chosen Stage 4 job | Narrow improvement that actually remains |
| --- | --- |
| **The Coal Waiver / Commerce** | The residents' appeal **was received but held until spring**; its reference and the objection to the delay have been recorded. It has **not** been approved |
| **The Door Count / Fieldcraft** | The city-recorded "empty" houses are known to be occupied; a street usable by coal carts was identified and indicated to residents. **No delivery is summoned** |
| **The Short Cart / Industry** | A smaller-than-promised coal delivery really reached Ward Six's ordinary receiving point, and its shortage was written down. **The shared box remains locked and the missing sacks aren't recovered** |

Neither the player nor the residents are rewarded with an entire winter supply of coal, a legal decision, a municipal budget reversal or control of the tenement. In both combat results, the **residents know the one finding** the player conveyed in Stage 5. Victory means Silas stopped threatening them for now; defeat means the player was driven off but the evidence and earlier limited help remain.

No speeches about an underground movement, no miraculous sympathy from Silas, no named landlord and no wider master plot. This is **a finished local episode**, not the main game's central mystery.

## I06-F — Normal Stage 7 clinic and universal Stage 8

After victory, the Inkbound reaches ordinary city care by normal travel. After defeat, the unnamed neighbour supports them toward the same service. The clinical provider uses the **standard 10-coin consultation/treatment** shared across all backgrounds; do not create an exclusive Inkbound clinic or make the guard take any cash.

- **Pay 10 coins once**, from the starter 20 to **10 remaining**, with actual injury-appropriate treatment and Health effects determined by the shared medical system. A perfect no-damage win **must not receive a fake wound** just to compel a dressing.
- The fight gives **0 coins, 0 ordinary XP, 0 combat XP, 0 loot or weapon**.
- The character returns to the **same shared rented home** and plays the identical already locked [Stage 8 rent notice and exact Millhand closing speech](../shared-home-rent-ending.md). No new Inkbound rent variant.
- Preserve the actual Silas combat result, the pre-fight position, the single Stage 3 +1 bonus and chosen branch finding, and the successful care/payment and home triggers. Never replay a defeated fight as a victory on load.
- Silas may be present in later work or story **only if separately designed**. No automatic standing change, new guard-faction reputation or citywide criminal flag is created here.
