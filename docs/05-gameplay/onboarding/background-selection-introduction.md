# CHOOSE YOUR BACKGROUND

You've lived and worked in Grand Cylinder City. How you've made your living has shaped what you know, whom you've dealt with, and the hardships you've endured.

**MILLHAND — Craftsmanship +3**

Long hours beside burning furnaces, shaping iron and repairing what others depend upon.

*Advantage:* Experienced in smithing, crafting and working with materials.

**BELLRUNNER — Industry +3**

Carrying messages, orders and shift notices through crowded streets, where being late can cost more than a day's wages.

*Advantage:* Skilled in hauling, deliveries and industrial work.

**SQUATTER — Fieldcraft +3**

Living on the city's neglected edges, gathering what little nature remains and learning where it's safe to tread.

*Advantage:* Experienced in foraging, scavenging and navigating dangerous terrain.

**STITCHER — Medicine +3**

Tending the sick and wounded in overcrowded infirmaries, where supplies are scarce and not everyone receives the care they need.

*Advantage:* Skilled in treating injuries, caring for the sick and handling medical work.

**SCRAPPER — Mechanics +3**

Dismantling condemned machinery and recovering useful metal from the city's rusting industrial remains.

*Advantage:* Experienced in repairing, dismantling and working with mechanical equipment.

**INKBOUND — Scholarship +3**

Copying council records, checking figures and handling documents that can determine whether someone receives their wages, coal or medicine.

*Advantage:* Skilled in investigating records, comparing evidence and uncovering discrepancies.

---

Each background grants **+3 levels in its associated Major Skill**.

Your choice determines your initial trade and the work you already know. It does not restrict which skills or professions you can learn later.

**Your past determines what you know. What you do next is up to you.**
