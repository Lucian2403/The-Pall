# Common Background Tutorial Structure

**Status: LOCKED structural design (2026-10-13).** This architecture applies to **all six** starting backgrounds: Millhand, Bellrunner, Squatter, Stitcher, Scrapper and Inkbound.

## Required E2E sequence

1. **Choose name, Male/Female portrait and background.** STR/DEX/INT/RES all start at 5; chosen background grants **+3 levels in one Major Skill**, before any tutorial quest.
2. **Start with identical kit for every background: Basic Clothes + one Crude Small Bag + 20 coins (LOCKED).** The actual item records/stats remain TBD. Begin in a background-specific persistent job hub and speak with its regular NPC job-giver.
3. **First unpaid tutorial work task — chosen background's Major Skill.** Run the normal reusable job preparation and gameplay interactions. The origin's exceptional story means this particular tutorial task gives **0 coins and 0 ordinary skill XP**. When repeated outside the tutorial, equivalent standard jobs retain their normal rewards.
4. **Unique twist after regular job interactions.** A background-specific incident prompts a meaningful choice. This event is not randomly repeated by the ordinary job system.
5. **Second unpaid tutorial work task — different Major Skill.** The incident choice branches to one of three logical tasks using normal reusable interactions appropriate to another Major Skill. **On committing to the second-task choice**, the character receives **exactly +1 level in that chosen second Major Skill**, once per character/tutorial, and no other tutorial skill award. The second task yields **0 coins and 0 ordinary XP**, just like the first. Millhand routes lead to Industry / Commerce / Scholarship. Standard non-tutorial jobs pay ordinary wages/XP.
6. **Two meaningful follow-up interactions after the second job,** bridging the story and impending hostile encounter. They should use reusable interaction frameworks (dialogue/inspection/approach), not a new minigame each.
7. **Short introductory PvE fight**, with an antagonist logically tied to the chosen background's story/route. The game uses **turn-based combat**, but its system and numerical balance will be defined separately. Fight is survivable and moderately challenging; there must be a non-blocking defeat outcome. **No extra coins, combat XP, skill levels, or item rewards** from the tutorial fight.
8. **City medical treatment/patch-up** using the regular Health/injury/care service interface. **The tutorial treatment costs exactly 10 coins**, including a defeat-recovery route; exact Health restoration, injury states and ordinary non-tutorial medical rates remain TBD.
9. **Return to the SAME shared starter rented home**, irrespective of background. No origin-specific hut/room variants or location/perk differences. The character now has **10 coins** (20 starting − 10 clinic, with no other tutorial rewards); **rent due: 150 coins as a PROVISIONAL figure** (shortfall 140). **The rent-due notice appears only at home after treatment** and ends this E2E tutorial. Payment deadline, collector, sanctions and housing upgrades remain TBD. This is economic pressure, **not** the overarching main storyline.

## Consistency rules

- Background-specific content varies by start location, recurring NPC, first job, eerie/dark incident, three-way choice, second-job specializations, antagonist and narrative detail.
- **Every NPC must eventually receive a personal in-world name (LOCKED).** Roles still appear as titles, but all six origin recurring job-givers now have approved names: **Guther Anvil** (Smithing Foreman), **Hector D. Smoke** (Dispatch Master), **Benny Ben** (Herb Dealer), **Claudia Cyka** (Matron, she/her), **Ignatius Smog** (Yard Foreman), **Erwin Buro** (Senior Copy Clerk). Names for additional NPCs are defined and approved individually; do not silently canonize invented names.
- **All backgrounds use the exact structural rhythm above**. Each leaves the tutorial with exactly 10 coins (absent unrelated optional spending, which the tutorial should not require), first +3 creation levels and one +1 second-skill level; route choices have comparable challenge/duration and no tutorial wage or XP imbalance.
- Real-time job and travel clocks continue during interactions according to [Jobs and Time](../jobs/jobs-and-time.md); do not force players to wait for late prompts.
- Combat is scripted **PvE** for onboarding, not an expansion of nonconsensual PvP rules; no special combat system is created only for tutorials.
- Tasks may introduce lore to the player without treating their character as someone who needs the basics of their life explained.
- The opening chapters are **self-contained jobs and incidents**, not the start of the game's central story. Do not promise systemic future ramifications unless authored.
- **Universal starting items and accommodation are LOCKED:** every origin and portrait starts with **Basic Clothes, one Crude Small Bag and 20 coins** and returns to the **same starter rented home/location**. Exact gear and home properties remain TBD. No origin-specific starter items, rooms, huts, rental prices or cosmetic variants are required.
- **Tutorial reward override (LOCKED 2026-10-14):** these SIX origin sequences grant **no wage, normal XP, combat XP or loot** for their tutorial work/fight. The only level changes are the **+3 Major Skill** at creation and **one +1 Major Skill** upon the second-task choice; never award the +1 multiple times on retries. Ordinary repeatable jobs and later combat keep their normal economy/XP systems. Do not mislead players by displaying a tutorial payout that will be withheld without explanation. The exceptional circumstances causing non-payment should be credibly written per background and need not be identical across origins.

## Shared housing and economic guardrails (LOCKED unless marked provisional)

- **One home, one room/hut implementation** for all six starting backgrounds: the same location, floor plan, functions, storage rules and rent. Exact address, home asset and storage amount are future implementation details; the backgrounds' different initial *job hubs* still stay unique.
- **Opening balance:** 20 coins. No coin rewards from the first/second tutorial tasks or fight; the story must explain nonpayment without teaching that every ordinary job is unpaid. **Clinic:** fixed 10 coins for the onboarding patch-up. **Home balance:** 10 coins. **Rent notice:** 150 coins **PROVISIONAL**, implying 140 coins still to earn.
- **Rent is revealed, not immediately demanded/collected during the tutorial.** Rent frequency, deadline, late consequences and when it becomes an interactive payment remain TBD. Normal reliable jobs must be available after the tutorial so the deficit can be earned.
- **Progression:** +3 creation levels in the origin's Major Skill; **+1 level at selection of the second task in its other Major Skill**. No extra skill XP for either tutorial task or introductory combat; this is not a standard XP amount disguised as a level.
- **Normal jobs are not changed:** although the origin's scripted copies withhold pay/XP, normal repeatable versions still pay and advance according to the ordinary job system. Keep the reused work interactions intact.

## Content authoring checklist per background

Start location (persistent) · NPC (recurring) · first task and generic interactions · unique twist · choice-driven second Major Skill task (+1 level) · two bridging interactions · motivated enemy · **10-coin treatment** · one shared home/**150-coin provisional rent** · no tutorial XP or money · defeat/retry behavior · asset/UI and persistent state references.
