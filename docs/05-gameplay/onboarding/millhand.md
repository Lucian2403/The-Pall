# Millhand — Starting Location and Opening Tutorial

**Status: Partially locked (2026-10-13).** Only Millhand has been specified in this detail. Other backgrounds will be addressed individually, not assumed from these choices.

## Locked setup

- **Background:** Millhand, associated Major Skill **Craftsmanship +3 levels** at creation. All four attributes STR / DEX / INT / RES start at 5, Health maximum starts at 50.
- **Portrait:** one of the two hardcoded **Millhand Male / Millhand Female** portraits, as chosen in character creation. Equipment overlays may change what appears.
- **Starting location:** a **noisy Smithing workshop in Grand Cylinder City**. This same workshop is the **generic hub for all Smithing jobs**, not a one-use tutorial backdrop. Its exact in-world name, layout and neighborhood are TBD.
- **First interaction:** **talk to the recurring Smithing job NPC** at the workshop. This NPC is the regular Smithing-work giver and pays coins and relevant job XP when the job is completed, including tutorial work. NPC name, appearance, pay formula and precise XP figures are TBD.
- **Starting inventory:** **Worker Clothes**, **the cheapest available Bag**, **20 coins**. These item labels are role descriptions only until the equipment/items sheet defines exact item IDs, quality, weights, capacity, protections and durability. No extra unique origin loot is required.
- **Introductory tutorial:** one short Millhand-related job/story segment. Its completion **reveals rent coming due**, bringing the player to the shared opening motivation. The character already belongs to this world; dialogue introduces lore to the player without amnesia/exposition.
- This opening should use existing interactive-job rules: inspect/prepare, make a meaningful decision, resolve work, get coins and XP. The tutorial should not be a passive wait and should fit roughly 2–5 minutes.

## Draft narrative pitch — PROPOSAL, not approved

**Working title: The Missing Stamp.**

The Smithing foreman gives the player a quick piecework job: inspect a batch of iron identification plates from a municipal labour contract, straighten a faulty plate, and re-stamp it. The player spots that the plate's identifying mark belongs to a worker officially recorded as deceased after a locked-door workshop fire, yet its inspection stamp is fresh.

The choice is small but uncomfortable:
- **Obey:** restamp the plate, finish quickly, and accept the foreman's mundane explanation about reused metal.
- **Question:** call out the mark and ask who signed the contract; the foreman is plainly uneasy.
- **Document:** note the mark for later before finishing the order; knowledge enters the journal rather than adding an undefined inventory item.

Every route resolves the same simple job and awards baseline Smithing-related job pay/XP. Choice changes dialogue and potentially a modest future hook; exact consequences are not specified. No required combat, crime system, new crafting recipe, new permanent faction reputation, or high-tier item.

On completion, the foreman gives the player the usual pay and passes along a landlord's notice or mentions a rent demand already waiting for them. The exact form, amount, due date and consequences are TBD. **The mystery and event details are not canon until explicitly approved.**
