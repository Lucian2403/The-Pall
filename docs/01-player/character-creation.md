# Character Creation — Phase One

Status: Partially agreed, 2026-10-13. Open design choices are distinguished from confirmed decisions.

## Agreed fields

- Character name.
- Modular stylized 2D portrait; not a fixed portrait roster or full-body character editor.
- Six **locked** background/origin names and social roles. Every chosen background grants **+3 levels in its associated Major Skill at character creation**, and provides **one small background-related tutorial quest**. Unique starter items are not required or defined yet.
- **All attributes fixed at STR 5, DEX 5, INT 5, RES 5 for every background.** No point distribution or background-based attribute changes (LOCKED).
- No age selection.
- Player identity and narrative framing are not yet established. A rent payment is a possible narrative hook, not a finalized obligation.

## Portrait modules

- 4 body silhouettes
- 10 faces
- 12 hairstyles
- 8 beard styles plus none
- 10 eye/brow options
- 4 skin colors
- 12 tops
- 8 outerwear variants
- No cosmetic accessories; equipment with gameplay functions is shown through equipped overlays.

Art direction: distinct, simplified 2D industrial illustration; graphic shapes; muted colors; consistent linework and anchor points. Art assets may be created with AI assistance but need manual consistency checks. 512 by 512 transparent layers are a proposed technical standard.

## Six backgrounds — agreed skills and tutorial scope

**LOCKED (2026-10-13):** six names and social origins; four attributes fixed at 5; +3 levels to exactly **one Major Skill** associated with the chosen background; one short background-related introductory tutorial quest.

| Background | Where they come from | Starting Major Skill bonus | Brief tutorial idea (proposed, not yet detailed) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Millhand** | Foundry and workshop labor | **Craftsmanship +3** | Help a worker assess and mend a simple production fault |
| **Bellrunner** | City messengers and shift couriers | **Industry +3** | Deliver a shift notice and learn the city's labour routes |
| **Squatter** | Families at the protected outskirts | **Fieldcraft +3** | Find a safe patch of useful plants just outside the gates |
| **Stitcher** | Clinic attendants and informal caregivers | **Medicine +3** | Assist with a routine minor injury and learn who receives care |
| **Scrapper** | Scrap-yard pickers and demolition crews | **Fieldcraft +3** | Identify and sort reusable scrap at a municipal salvage pile |
| **Inkbound** | Records keepers and ledger copyists | **Scholarship +3** | Investigate a discrepancy in a city work or ration ledger |

**Implementation rule:** The +3 is applied at character creation to the **Major Skill level**, not to a subskill or attribute, and is not contingent on completing the tutorial quest. Other Major Skills begin at the same common baseline for every character. Squatter and Scrapper intentionally share Fieldcraft; their identities, brief tutorials and eventual contacts differ.

**Tutorial rule:** The origin-specific segment should be brief (target about 2–5 minutes), grounded in local lore and a meaningful interaction. It then joins the **shared** first-session story; origins do not create six separate main questlines, unique professions or ongoing mandatory origin missions. The exact NPCs, objectives, dialogue and incidental rewards remain to be authored.

**Inventory rule:** Do **not** assign unique background items merely to fill a character-creation column. The already-agreed common starter direction is worn basic clothing, a small carrying bag and modest starting money (exact item definitions and amounts TBD). If suitable, properly specified inventory items become available later, optional origin-specific starter variations may be introduced with comparable value; no such variations are locked now.

A background has real value through early Major Skill progress, a distinctive social perspective and a short tailored introduction, while all players retain unrestricted long-term progression.
