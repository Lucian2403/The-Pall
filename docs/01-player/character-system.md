# Character System

## Core attributes

- **STR — Strength:** physical potential, carrying, heavy jobs/equipment, melee and heavy-tool training.
- **DEX — Dexterity:** coordination, firearms, movement, locks and delicate technical work.
- **INT — Intellect:** learning and reasoning, research, medicine, chemistry, engineering and investigation.
- **RES — Resolve:** endurance under strain, mental resilience and difficult training.

Initial target: STR 5 / DEX 5 / INT 5 / RES 5.

## Progression rule

Attribute → unlocks training/opportunity → training improves skill → skill improves performance.

Attributes should not automatically turn into large combat bonuses.

## Health

New characters begin with **50 maximum Health**.

Long-term natural progression may approach **100**, but 100 should be exceptional.

Injuries can lower the effective Health Ceiling. Example: a character with base maximum 65 and a broken limb may temporarily be limited to 55/55, alongside limb-specific penalties.

## Physical state

- **Stamina:** spendable physical reserve.
- **Fatigue:** harmful state created by overexertion, especially when acting at low or zero Stamina.

## Mental state

- **Composure:** spendable mental reserve.
- **Stress:** harmful state created by mental overload, danger and acting at low or zero Composure.

## Focus

Focus is **derived, not spent**.

It is influenced by Composure, Stress, Fatigue, pain, injuries, Hunger, Thirst, medicine and other conditions.

Low Focus harms precision actions such as shooting, surgery, research, lock work and delicate crafting.

## Hunger and Thirst

Hunger matters both inside and outside cities.

Thirst is automatically maintained during ordinary life inside safe cities. Outside the city it becomes an active survival need.

Special city crises may temporarily make water management relevant inside the city.

## Pall and Ash resistance

Attributes do not directly provide meaningful Pall/Ash resistance.

Survival depends mainly on:
- respirators and filters;
- sealed clothing;
- appropriate equipment;
- medicine and chemical treatments;
- rare foods and consumables.

## Carrying

Three layers:

1. **Natural Carry Capacity** — improved through STR-gated training, work and skills.
2. **Carried storage** — backpacks, frames and containers.
3. **Transport capacity** — carts, steam-carriages and eventually trains.

## Equipment slots

- Head
- Face
- Torso
- Hands
- Pants
- Feet
- Back / Backpack
- Primary Weapon
- Secondary Weapon
- Respiratory Filter
- Accessory Slots

Animals such as horses are deferred because they imply their own feeding, disease, injury, Pall exposure and logistics systems.
