# Fastener Set — Phase One

**Status: LOCKED (2026-10-12)**

Fastener Set is a general-purpose manufactured joining component: an abstract bundle of small steel bolts, screws, washers, retaining pins and similar hardware used to hold an assembly together. It is not a precision mechanism, and does not represent an individual bolt. Add it **only where a specific named recipe or repair has a justified need**, not to all manufactured items indiscriminately.

## Recipe

**0.25 kg Steel → 8 Fastener Sets**

Bulk Steel is measured by mass and supports fractional-kg inventory and recipe operations. A player may buy or salvage Steel; Refining 20 to make Steel is not an additional Smithing 5 prerequisite.

## Named demand

- **Basic Firing Mechanism:** 2 Fastener Sets.
- **Refined Firing Mechanism:** 2 Fastener Sets.
- **Precision Firing Mechanism:** **4** Fastener Sets.
- **Gear Assembly:** 8 Fastener Sets under the **revised** recipe.

The Fastener Set is the universal mechanical fastening ingredient where needed; **Copper Fitting** retains its specialized pressure-connection purpose. Do not multiply fastener recipes into different technological tiers without a concrete gameplay justification.
